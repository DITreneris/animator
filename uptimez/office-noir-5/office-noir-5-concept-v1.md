# Office Noir 5: concept v1 (25 Nov, Tomas, /grill-me)

## Whose dream

**Mom's.** The round so far: Noir 1 Don, Noir 2 Sofía, Noir 3 the Client, Noir 4 Pam. Mom has never dreamed; her canon secret (STORYLINE / 19 Nov) is that she wants Pam to become Don's wife, and Pam does not know.

She is the dream's **elderly amateur sleuth**, a retired lady everyone underestimates: an archetype of the Miss Marple kind, **our own version**. No name, look or book detail from Christie (her estate owns them); her props come from Mom's canon: the knitting, the one forgotten curler, the handbag.

## The umbrella (Tomas, 25 Nov 19:40: "Mom has an umbrella, the gag: one Mom, and behind the umbrella a completely different one, a super agent")

| | Pick |
|---|---|
| The switch | **when nobody looks**: the umbrella passes in front of her like a curtain; behind it she is the agent while Sofía's back is turned, and the instant Sofía looks round she is the frail pensioner again. It repeats at every place, faster each time (the chase's engine) |
| The agent | sleek black, dark glasses, upright and fast; she keeps her marks: the forgotten curler and the red knitted hat (the one red) |
| The umbrella | **black** (the red stays the hat and its thread) |
| Its tricks (all four), one per place | **periscope** in the handle: watching Sofía round the alley's corner / the traffic light; **hook**: the crook catches the garage roof and swings her up; **glider**: off the garage roofs down to the crossing; **shield**: against the crossing's headlights and against Sofía's glance |

The route with the tricks: alley (periscope, the hat's thread catches) → garages (hook up onto the roofs) → glide to the crossing → crossing (shield: headlights, Sofía turns: granny) → traffic light (periscope) → the church (the umbrella closes; the wrap comes off; Pam in white).

## Weather

**It rains** (Tomas: "it rains, of course, she's a granny"). Reuse Noir 4's look: one thin rain layer, wet stones with glints, the lamps smeared down the road (`on4_weather`; copy, don't import across films). The photos are dry: the sets get the rain added. The umbrella is open for a reason, so the granny is never suspicious.

## Season and the busker (Tomas, 25 Nov; revised 20:55: "Don can't be Santa, it will already be after Christmas")

| | Pick |
|---|---|
| The occasion | **a winter night, no occasion**: rain in January; the street light arches still hang (they do into January) but nothing says Christmas. (Replaces "a Christmas special".) No Santa, no carols |
| The trumpet busker | **Don in his own coat and hat**, busking for the ring, his hat on the cobbles for coins; Mom passes her own son without knowing him. (Replaces "Don as Santa".) His trumpet can carry the score: a spy theme while she is the agent, an innocent tune the instant Sofía turns |
| The avenue | **the finishing straight** before the wrapped church; the light arch over the road reads as a wedding arch. A generic cathedral square, not a copy of the real one |
| Brands | the busker's case lettering and the optician's sign are left out |

Route now: alley → garages → crossing → traffic light → the busker's square (Don with the trumpet) → the avenue under the light arch → the wrapped church.
