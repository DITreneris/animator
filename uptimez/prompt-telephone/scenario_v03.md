# Uptime Inc. — "Prompt Telephone" (v03)

**Format:** 15-second vertical loop (9:16), crude paper-cutout style
**Series:** Uptime Inc., an AI office sitcom
**Status:** v03, ready for a timed storyboard A/B test
**Previous version:** `prompt_telephone_scenario_v01.md` (kept for reference)

---

## 0. What changed in v03, and why

The review said "an amusing explainer, not a funny cartoon": viewers understood the joke before the punchline. Four reviews in a row have found the same pattern: **clear, but no surprising human decision.**

| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| **The show is about ridiculous people, not AI mistakes** | The strongest note from every review. The AI does exactly what it's told, and the humans fail. |
| **Mistakes escalate:** PAM *edits* a word out, RUSH *mistypes* one, and the AI *takes it literally* | v01 repeated the same mistake twice ("and then"). Now each person fails differently and worse. |
| **The laptop is deadpan**, not cute | An innocent, expressionless AI makes the humans look worse, which is funnier. |
| **The punchline lands on the top:** DON: "...Finally. Something I understand." | It reveals that the boss is the most clueless person in the chain. |
| **Cut:** the 2.4M likes counter, RUSH fainting, PAM's "My idea.", MAX | The counter rewarded the mistake and lost the satire. The rest was clutter in 15 seconds. |
| **Kept:** the falling words, and the cat as background only | The falling words are the most original visual. The cat is cute, but a smile isn't a laugh. |

## 1. The idea in one sentence

The CEO's order travels down the chain, and each person ruins it a little more. The innocent AI delivers exactly what reached it, and the CEO finally understands something.

**The joke:** the problem was never the AI. Every human in the chain is the problem, and the boss most of all.

## 3. The core visual: words fall out of the order

The order is a speech bubble made of **separate word tiles**. As it passes down the chain, tiles physically fall out and land on the floor with a clunk, where they stay as evidence.

```
DON  : [ BOLD! ][ AUTHENTIC! ][ VIRAL! ]
PAM  : [ BOLD! ]  ..AUTHENTIC falls.. [ VIRAL! ]        (clunk)
RUSH : [ BOLD ]  ..VIRAL falls..                          (clunk)
RUSH types:  A: "Bold."      B: "Bald."   <- the typo, visible on screen
LAPTOP:      A: BOLD         B: a photo of DON's bald head, captioned BALD
```

## 4. Scenario, beat by beat

The first 10.5 s are identical in A and B, except for **one letter** typed in beat 4 and the result shown in beat 5.

| # | Time (s) | Picture | Line / sound | Beat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–2.5 | **DON** slams the printout on PAM's desk. His bubble shows three big tiles: BOLD! AUTHENTIC! VIRAL! | DON: **"Make it bold! Authentic! Viral!"** Slam. | The order |
| 2 | 2.5–4.5 | **PAM** turns to RUSH, pointing. She frowns at AUTHENTIC and flicks it out of her bubble. It falls and clunks on the floor. | PAM: **"Bold and viral!"** Clunk. | *But* PAM "edits" |
| 3 | 4.5–7.0 | **RUSH**, panicking, at his desk with LAPTOP. VIRAL slides out of his bubble. | RUSH: **"...Bold."** Clunk. | *Therefore* only one word survives |
| 4 | 7.0–8.5 | Close-up of the chat box as RUSH types fast. **A:** `Bold.` **B:** `Bald.` (the typo sits in plain sight). Enter. | Key taps, Enter | *But* RUSH types it wrong (B) |
| 5 | 8.5–10.5 | **Reveal on the LAPTOP screen.** **A:** one giant word, **BOLD**. **B:** a photo of **DON's bald head**, with the caption **BALD**. The laptop's face stays deadpan. | Pop, then silence | *Therefore* the AI does exactly what reached it |
| 6 | 10.5–12.0 | Wide shot. Everyone freezes as DON leans to the screen. PAM and RUSH are terrified. On the floor: AUTHENTIC (with CACHE asleep on it) and VIRAL. | Clock ticks | Tension: who gets yelled at? |
| 7 | 12.0–15.0 | Close-up of DON, softened, almost moved: | DON: **"...Finally. Something I understand."** | *But* the boss is delighted, and it loops back to his next order |

**Word count:** 13 spoken words. The punchline line holds for about 2.5 s.

**Loop:** the last frame is DON satisfied; the first frame is DON slamming a new order. It reads as "and he does it again tomorrow".

## 5. What we're testing: A vs B

| | **A, "BOLD"** | **B, "BALD"** |
|---|---|---|
| What reaches the AI | The word the chain kept | RUSH's typo |
| Result on screen | A post that says BOLD | A photo of DON's bald head: BALD |
| Who looks stupid | The chain (lost words) | The chain, RUSH's typo, and DON loves a picture of his own head |
| Strength | Clean, safe, easy to read | Bigger surprise, and the punchline is about DON |
| Risk | Viewers predict it from beat 3 | The typo might be missed: `Bald.` must be big and readable for at least 0.6 s |

**Hypothesis:** B gets more unprompted laughs, because the result is something viewers didn't predict. **Only the test decides.**

---

## 6. Sound plan

| Moment | Sound |
|---|---|
| DON's slam | A heavy desk thump |
| Each falling word | A wooden **clunk** with a bounce |
| RUSH typing | Fast key taps, then Enter |
| Reveal | A clean "pop", then silence |
| Freeze | A clock tick per second |
| DON's last line | Voice only, no music |

**Voices:** record DON yourself if possible. Shouting the first line and then softening on the last is the whole performance, and computer voices flatten both.

---

## 7. Test plan

Use the v4.1 test-pack method:

- Show **A to 5 people and B to 5 different people**, on a phone, looped 3 times, without explaining anything.
- Count **unprompted laughs only**. A polite "yeah, funny" doesn't count.
- Ask: *"What happened?"*, *"What was the funniest moment?"*, and, for B only, *"What did RUSH type?"*

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| They can explain what happened | 4 of 5 |
| Unprompted laugh | 3 of 5 |
| They name the funny moment | 4 of 5 |
| B only: they noticed the typo | 4 of 5, or else enlarge the typo |

**If both fail:** don't polish. Write 20 alternative *human decisions* for beats 4–7, pick the 2 best, and test again.

---

## 8. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| The falling tiles are too small to read on a phone | Each tile at least 40 px tall; the falling tile is the biggest |
| B's typo is missed | `Bald.` typed in big letters; hold the chat box 0.6 s before Enter |
| "Finally. Something I understand." reads as sarcasm | DON visibly softens: smile, hand on chest |
| Too much in 15 s | Four characters plus a silent cat. No counter, no extra gags. |
