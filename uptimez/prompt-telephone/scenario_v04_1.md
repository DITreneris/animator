## The joke in one line

One human mistake that keeps escalating, one AI correction that the human deliberately rejects, and one boss who loves the disaster for the worst possible reason.

## What changed from v03 (after the v04 review)

| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| **New:** the laptop asks "Did you mean: Bold?" and RUSH **slams NO** | The strongest beat: a human confidently chooses the mistake |
| **New punchline:** "...Finally. A post about me." | DON's vanity explains his delight, and makes the bald photo essential |
| **The photo shows DON's face** (the same bald head and tufts as the character) | Viewers instantly think "that's DON" |
| **One quick action per mistake:** PAM strikes and flicks; RUSH sprays and shoves | v04's tongs and bin were a lot of animation for a small joke |
| **Laptop beats merged into one continuous shot** (5.0–10.5 s) | 6 shots instead of 9, and the key sequence BOLD?, then NO, then BALD gets room |
| **DON's reaction is 3.3 s** with a hand on his heart and a tear | His face carries the laugh, not only the line |
| **The deadpan laptop rolls its eyes at the end** | The AI judges the humans, as a button. It doesn't compete with the punchline. |
| **One office, an accent-colour wall per character** (DON red, PAM purple, RUSH teal) | Consistent world, quick character recognition |
| **Cut:** the freeze wide shot, the cat, tongs and bin | Clutter that competed with the reveal and DON's reaction |

**Not measured, needs people:** whether it's funny, whether viewers notice the NO, and how the voices land. The computer voices flatten DON's shout and his soft line, and that's where your own recording would help most.

## Test protocol (corrected after the review)

1. **v04.1 to 5 people, v03-B to 5 *different* people.** At least 2 non-technical per group, and nobody who has seen any version.
2. **Show it once.** Record the first reaction: unprompted laugh, smile, or nothing.
3. Then ask: *"What happened?"* / *"What did RUSH press?"* / *"What was the funniest moment?"*
4. **Only then** loop it, and note any repeat-view reactions separately.

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Explains what happened | 4 of 5 |
| Noticed RUSH pressed NO | 4 of 5, the key causal beat |
| Recognised DON in the photo | 4 of 5 |
| Unprompted laugh on the first viewing | 3 of 5 |

**Five per version can show confusion, but can't prove a winner.** If the results are close, test more people before declaring v04.1 better.

## If it fails

| Sign | Fix |
|---|---|
| The NO is missed | Give the dialog 2.0 s and show RUSH's face (smug) as he presses |
| DON isn't recognised in the photo | Include his red tie and suit collar in the photo |
| A smile, not a laugh, at "A post about me." | Try the alternative line "Promote whoever wrote this." |
| Confusion before the laptop shot | Cut the sanitiser beat first. Never cut BOLD?, NO, BALD or DON's reaction. |
