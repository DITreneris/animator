# Uptime Inc. — "Prompt Telephone"

**Format:** 15-second vertical loop (9:16), crude paper-cutout style
**Series:** Uptime Inc., an AI office sitcom
**Status:** concept, ready for a timed storyboard and the viewer test
**Source:** our own "kick the cat" chain meme (promptanatomy.app)

---

## 1. The idea in one sentence

The CEO's instruction is passed down the chain of command and loses words at every step, until the AI receives one word, takes it literally, and the CEO loves the result.

**The joke:** the more pressure flows down the chain, the less meaning reaches the bottom, and nobody at the top notices.

---

## 2. Why it works

| Ingredient | How it shows up |
|---|---|
| **Familiar in 2 seconds** | A boss yelling at a manager, who yells at an employee. Everyone has seen it. |
| **Literal absurdity** | "Make it BOLD" becomes a post that just says **BOLD**, in bold. |
| **Confidently wrong** | The CEO calls the result "Genius." |
| **But / therefore** | Each person *therefore* passes the order on, *but* drops a word, *therefore* the AI gets one word, *but* the CEO loves it. |
| **Visible punchline** | One giant word on a screen works with the sound off. |
| **One memorable gag** | The post that just says "BOLD." |
| **Brand fit** | It's literally about prompting, the core of promptanatomy.app. |

**Our twist on the original meme:** the original ends on a weak label ("Another Generic Facebook Post"). Ours ends on a picture (BOLD) and then a reversal (the CEO loves it).

---

## 3. Cast

| Badge | Character | Look | Personality | Role in this episode |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **DON · CEO** *(new)* | The boss | Biggest character, dark suit, loud red tie, always gripping a crumpled printout | Shouts in capitals, wants everything "viral" | Starts the chain |
| **PAM · PROJECT MGR** | The manager | Purple blazer, bob haircut, green lanyard | Confident, passes things on fast, edits as she goes | Drops "authentic" |
| **RUSH · SALES** | The anxious executor | White shirt, red tie, sweat drop | Panics, simplifies everything | Drops "viral", types one word |
| **LAPTOP** *(new)* | The AI | An ordinary laptop with a face on the screen: two dot eyes and a smile, a chat box below | Eager, literal, helpful | Writes "BOLD" |
| **MAX · DEV** | The sensible one | Grey hoodie, glasses | Quietly sees the absurdity | Background witness: one look to camera |
| **CACHE** *(optional cameo)* | Office cat | Black cutout cat, silent | Unbothered | Sleeps on the dropped word "AUTHENTIC" |

**Design rules:**
- LAPTOP is a real, recognisable laptop, not a robot, mascot or symbol. No brand names or logos on it.
- DON has to read as "the boss" without his badge: the biggest, loudest character, and the only one in a dark suit.
- Every face must stay visible in every panel. Monitors, arms and bubbles mustn't cover them.

---

## 4. Setting

**Uptime Inc., open-plan office.** One continuous space, read from left to right like a chain:

```
[ DON's corner office door ] -> [ PAM's desk ] -> [ RUSH's desk + LAPTOP ] -> [ wall screen: LIKES counter ]
```

**The planted detail:** a big wall screen labelled **LIKES** shows a sad **3** from the first shot. It sits in plain sight and nobody mentions it until the end, when it rockets to **2.4M**.

---

## 5. Scenario, beat by beat

The core visual gag: **the speech bubble physically loses words as the order passes down.** The dropped word falls out of the bubble and lands on the floor with a clunk. Viewers *see* the instruction shrinking, even with the sound off.

| # | Time (s) | Picture | Line / sound | Beat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–2.5 | **DON** slams the crumpled printout onto PAM's desk. The LIKES screen behind shows **3**. | DON: **"Make it BOLD. AUTHENTIC. VIRAL!"** Desk slam. | Setup, at full volume |
| 2 | 2.5–4.5 | **PAM** spins to RUSH and jabs a finger. Her bubble shows 3 words, and **"AUTHENTIC" falls off**, bouncing on the floor. | PAM: **"Make it bold and viral!"** Clunk. | *Therefore* she passes it on, *but* a word is lost |
| 3 | 4.5–6.5 | **RUSH**, panicking, turns to the LAPTOP. His bubble starts as "Bold. Viral." and **"VIRAL" falls off.** CACHE wanders over and sits on "AUTHENTIC". | RUSH: **"...Bold."** Clunk. | *Therefore* he passes it on, *but* only one word survives |
| 4 | 6.5–8.0 | Close-up of the LAPTOP. The chat box shows RUSH typing **Bold.** and pressing Enter. The laptop's face lights up and typing dots bounce. | Key taps, enter, a happy "ding" | The cause is shown on screen |
| 5 | 8.0–10.0 | **Reveal:** the laptop screen fills with a single post: **BOLD**, in huge, heavy letters. LAPTOP beams proudly. | A soft "pop", then silence | *Therefore* the AI takes it literally. **The visual punchline.** |
| 6 | 10.0–11.5 | Everyone freezes. DON leans in, very close to the screen. RUSH is sweating, PAM holding her breath. MAX looks straight into the camera. | Silence, a clock tick | Tension: who gets yelled at? |
| 7 | 11.5–13.0 | DON, softly, almost moved: | DON: **"...Genius."** | *But* the CEO loves it. **The reversal.** |
| 8 | 13.0–15.0 | The **LIKES** wall screen rockets from 3 to **2.4M**. PAM steps in front of RUSH: | PAM: **"My idea."** Counter whirr, a cheer. RUSH faints out of frame. | The office-satire sting. Loops back to DON slamming the next printout. |

### Why the loop works

The last shot leaves DON with a new success and a new appetite. The opening shot is DON slamming a printout and demanding more. On a second watch it reads as "and here we go again", which is the office cycle.

### Word count and timing

- Spoken words: 5 + 5 + 1 + 1 + 2 = **14 words**, well under the budget of about 3 words per second.
- The punchline, BOLD on screen, holds for **2 seconds** (8.0–10.0).
- The silent beat (10.0–11.5) is deliberate. It's the comic pause before the reversal, so keep it.

## 6. The key visual: the shrinking bubble

This is the gag that makes the episode work with the sound off:

```
DON:   [ BOLD.  AUTHENTIC.  VIRAL! ]
PAM:   [ bold   ~~AUTHENTIC~~  viral ]   -> "AUTHENTIC" drops to the floor (clunk)
RUSH:  [ bold   ~~VIRAL~~ ]               -> "VIRAL" drops to the floor (clunk)
LAPTOP:  input "Bold."  ->  output  ███ BOLD ███
```

- Dropped words fall as **physical cutout letters** and stay on the floor for the rest of the video, as visible evidence.
- In the last shot, the floor shows **AUTHENTIC** (with CACHE asleep on it) and **VIRAL** lying abandoned.

## 7. Ending options to test

Panels 1–7 stay identical. Only panel 8 changes, so the test compares one thing at a time.

| Option | Panel 8 | Strength | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A (main)** | Counter rockets to 2.4M. PAM: "My idea." RUSH faints. | Two hits: absurd success, then a credit-stealing sting | Busy: three things in 2 seconds |
| **B** | Counter rockets. DON turns to the camera: "Now make it... AUTHENTIC." He picks the dropped word up off the floor. | Strong loop, and the dropped word pays off | Needs the dropped word to have been noticed |
| **C** | Counter rockets. LAPTOP, proudly, writes a second post: **VIRAL**. | The AI escalates its own literalness | Weaker human reaction |

**Pick for the first test: A.** If viewers find panel 8 too busy, cut RUSH fainting.

---

## 8. Sound plan

| Moment | Sound |
|---|---|
| DON's slam | A heavy desk thump |
| Each dropped word | A wooden **clunk** plus a small bounce |
| RUSH typing | 4–5 key taps, then Enter |
| LAPTOP thinking | Soft typing-dot blips |
| BOLD reveal | A clean "pop", then **silence** |
| Freeze | A single clock tick per second |
| "Genius." | No music, voice only |
| Counter rocket | A rising whirr, slot-machine dings, a short office cheer |
| Music | A light office groove up to the reveal. It cuts out at the reveal and returns with the counter. |

**Voices:** record them yourself if you can, especially DON's shouted opening line and his whispered "...Genius." Computer voices will flatten both.

---

## 9. Captions and on-screen text

- Every spoken line appears as a speech bubble, in Lilita One, at least 64 px at 1080 wide.
- The **BOLD** reveal fills at least 60% of the laptop screen and is readable at thumbnail size.
- The **LIKES** counter appears in panel 1 at a readable size (planted) and reads **2.4M** at the end.
- No brand names: no real AI product, social network or logo. The post screen is a generic feed card.

---

## 10. Test plan

Use the v4.1 test pack method:

1. **Round 1 (pitch):** one sentence plus one crude drawing of LAPTOP showing "BOLD". Ask, *"What do you think happens?"*
2. **Round 2 (timed slideshow):** 8 panels at the real timing above, with scratch voices and the clunks. Show it to 5 people who've never seen it, including at least 2 non-technical.

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| They can explain what happened | 4 of 5 |
| Unprompted laugh | 3 of 5 |
| They name the funny moment (expected: BOLD or "Genius.") | 4 of 5 |
| They noticed the words falling off | Record it, as a check on readability |

**What would make it fail:**
- Viewers don't notice the words dropping, so the BOLD result seems random. Fix: make the dropped words bigger and slower, and add the clunk.
- "Genius." reads as sarcasm. Fix: DON visibly moved, hand on heart.
- The LIKES counter isn't noticed in panel 1. Fix: give it a 0.5 s glance in the opening shot.

---

## 11. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Too many characters (5) for 15 s | DON, PAM and RUSH carry the chain. MAX and CACHE are silent background extras. |
| The bubble gag is hard to read on a phone | The dropped word is the biggest word in its bubble and falls slowly (0.5 s) |
| Predictable once the chain starts | The surprise isn't the chain. It's the literal BOLD, then "Genius." |
| Looks like the original meme's art | Our own cutout style and characters only. No 1950s illustration or real brands. |
