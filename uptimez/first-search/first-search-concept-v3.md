# UptimeZ Inc. — Episode 10: *First Search* — concept v3

Status: **concept, for review.** Nothing is built. v3 takes in the showrunner audit of concept v2 (17 Nov) and Tomas's choice to keep Mom's role. v1 and v2 are kept. Canon: [`../STORYLINE.md`](../STORYLINE.md). Built on episode 9 as finished ([`../the-onboarding/the-onboarding-script-v10.md`](../the-onboarding/the-onboarding-script-v10.md)).

**Target:** 42–45 s at 9:16, seven beats, ten lines.

## The principle for this episode

**Don wins the technical argument and loses the human one, in the same shot.**

Every want meets at one moment, the green-zero reveal:
- the Client wants his plan;
- Don wants Sofía's admiration;
- Sofía wants the Client's admiration;
- Pam wants Don to see her real work;
- the robot wants compliance;
- Mom wants her boy protected.

## Beat by beat

### Beat 1: The debt (0–5 s)

- **Frame 0:** the wide, with the screen's search bar empty, his photograph, Mom at her desk by the door, and Pam at the back with loose blue strips. The last frame matches it.
- **Action:** the glass slides; the Client walks in holding up his phone, **FRI · BANK**.
- **CLIENT:** "Friday, the bank. I need my blueprint."
- **Insert:** Pam's hands, a blue strip with a bay number, tape.
- **Reaction:** Don's sales smile switches on. Behind him, Mom straightens in her chair, proud.

### Beat 2: The upgrade (5–11 s)

- **Action:** the tablet: **UPGRADE · PREMIUM · GUARANTEED BY FRIDAY**.
- **DON:** "Premium. Guaranteed by Friday."
- **Insert:** the Client's oily thumb on the tick (the framing of episode 9's payment). A soft *ka-ching*.

### Beat 3: The red zero (11–16 s)

- **Action:** the robot types "blueprint". **Client POV:** push in on the screen. **0 RESULTS**, red, by about 12 s, and the robot's paddle snaps up red.
- **CLIENT:** "Is my blueprint even in there?"

### Beat 4: The mistake, and the two thumbs (16–25 s)

- **SOFÍA**, to the Client, confident, meaning to impress him: "Let's present it as a clean result."
- **DON**, lit up, to the robot: "Can you make the zero green?"
- **ROBOT:** "Interface change. Human approval required."
- **DON:** "Approved."
- **The match cut:** CU, Don's thumb presses APPROVED → CU, Pam's thumb presses tape onto a blue strip.
- **Screen:** the zero turns **green**; the paddle snaps up green; a cheerful chime cuts across the room.

### Beat 5: The reveal, one run of looks (25–31 s)

This is the episode, played with no lines:
1. **Don turns to Sofía** for applause.
2. **Sofía** is giving the Client a small, confident smile: she thinks she rescued it.
3. **The Client** leans toward the screen and looks for his plan. Nothing. He looks back at Don and **holds**.
4. **Sofía's smile dies.**
5. **Don** sees her face change and beams: he takes it for admiration.
6. **Pam**, large in the foreground with Don and Sofía small behind her, looks up at Don, wanting him to see the plan in her hands. He's watching Sofía. She goes back to the tape.

### Beat 6: The confrontation, and Mom (31–38 s)

- **CLIENT**, slowly: "You said I'd never lose anything again."
- **DON**, proudly, at the green zero: "We fixed the red."
- **Low angle:** the Client towers over Don. Above them, the photograph. Don glances up at it, as if it might confirm his success.
- **Mom** rises from her desk by the door. She's watched it all.
- **MOM:** "Young man."
- **Reaction:** the Client steps back, a well-brought-up man who doesn't argue with someone's mother. Don steps back too, by reflex: he's heard that tone all his life.
- **Reaction:** Sofía sees her CEO's mother rescue him. Pam sees it too.

### Beat 7: The consequence (38–45 s)

- **Action:** the Client leaves through the glass door.
- **Flash, half a second:** his phone, **100% COMPLETE**, *approved interface changes*.
- **CU:** Pam presses another strip into place. Bay 6.
- **Final wide:** the green zero, his framed face, and Pam's plan nearly whole with **a gap at 7**. We've already learned each of them. Mom sits back down, satisfied.
- **The loop:** cut to frame 0: the empty search bar, a few loose strips on Pam's desk.

## Gates

**G0 series check:**
- what Don wants: Sofía's admiration;
- who takes it: the Client's silence, then his mother;
- the ironic surface: a colour fix, a trophy photograph, a Friday guarantee;
- the iconic image: **the green zero beside the framed first client**, and the two thumbs;
- someone who matters sees him fail: Sofía, Pam and Mom.

**G1 protect list:**
1. The red zero by about 12 s.
2. The green zero and the two thumbs.
3. The run of looks: Don → Sofía → the Client → Sofía's smile dying → Don beaming.
4. "You said I'd never lose anything again." / "We fixed the red." under the photograph.
5. "Young man.", and two men stepping back for two different reasons.
