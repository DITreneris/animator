# UptimeZ Inc. — Episode 10: *First Search* — concept v1

Status: **concept, for review.** Nothing is built. It develops [`first-search-premise-v3.md`](first-search-premise-v3.md) into beats, shots, draft lines and sound. Canon: [`../STORYLINE.md`](../STORYLINE.md) v3.1. Rules: [`../CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md).

**Target:** 40–45 s at 9:16, 1080×1920, six beats. Real timing comes from the voice takes.

## The idea

A garage owner needs his blueprint for a manufacturer on Friday. The office sells him a Friday guarantee, finds nothing, and fixes the colour of nothing. **One green zero hurts three people three ways:**
- Don is proud of it.
- Sofía is embarrassed by it, because it came from her words.
- The Client can't believe it.

Only the audience knows the blueprint is blue confetti in the bin, three feet away.

## Beat by beat

Each beat has an **action** shot and a **reaction** shot (gate G2). Sizes: WS wide, MS medium, MCU medium close-up, CU close-up, INS insert.

### Beat 1: The hook (0–4 s)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Frame 0** | The big screen, close: an empty search bar, cursor blinking. It's also the last frame of the loop, so frame 0 is readable and moves within the first second |
| **Action** | MS: the Client walks in, a head taller than everyone, and puts his phone on the table showing **FRI · MANUFACTURER** |
| **Line** | CLIENT: "Friday. I need my blueprint." |
| **Insert** | INS, CU: the shredder bin. Blue strips, half a gear. One second, no sound cue, never mentioned |
| **Reaction** | MCU: Don, a salesman's smile switching on |
| **Sound** | Office hum, the door, his work boots |

### Beat 2: Don's win (4–10 s)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Action** | MS: Don slides the tablet across, and the price card fills it: **PREMIUM · GUARANTEED BY FRIDAY** |
| **Line** | DON: "Premium. Guaranteed by Friday." |
| **Action** | CU: the Client taps PAY and leaves an oily thumbprint on the glass |
| **Sound** | A soft register *ka-ching* |
| **Reaction 1** | MCU, over Don's shoulder: he turns to Sofía for the applause |
| **Reaction 2** | MCU: Sofía, not looking at Don. She's smiling at the Client |
| **Reaction 3** | MCU: Don straightens his vest, his silent signature, and leans back in, trying harder |

**Why it matters:** a real sale, sincerely made; Don's win comes before his failure, as canon requires. The turn to Sofía shows the triangle with no words.

### Beat 3: The disaster (10–15 s)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Action** | WS: the robot types "blueprint" into the big screen. All four faces turn toward it |
| **Screen** | **0 RESULTS**, in red, **by about 12 s** |
| **Sound** | A flat error tone |
| **Line** | CLIENT: "Is my blueprint even in there?" |
| **Line** | DON, sincerely, pointing at the screen: "It's AI-powered." |
| **Reaction** | MCU: the Client checks his watch, his silent signature |

### Beat 4: The engine (15–25 s)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Line** | SOFÍA, smoothly, to the Client: "Let's present it as a clean result." |
| **Action** | MCU: Don, lit up, turns to the robot |
| **Line** | DON: "Can you make the zero green?" |
| **Line** | ROBOT: "Interface change. Human approval required." |
| **Line** | DON: "Approved." |
| **Screen** | CU: the zero turns **green**. A small tick: **Change applied** |
| **Sound** | A cheerful success chime, the wrong sound for the moment |
| **Reaction** | CU: Sofía's face in two seconds: satisfaction → recognition → horror → professional smile |

**Why it matters:** two desires collide. Sofía's careful wording plus Don's literal mind is the comic mechanism. She never asked for a fake; he never knowingly lies.

### Beat 5: The confrontation (25–33 s)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Line** | CLIENT, slowly: "Did you find my blueprint?" |
| **Line** | DON, proudly, gesturing at the green zero: "We fixed the red." |
| **Action** | Low angle: the Client stands and leans over Don, a head taller. The status reverses (power-angle shot) |
| **Action** | WS through the glass door: **Mom appears.** She doesn't come in |
| **Reaction** | MS: one look from Mom. The Client steps back, and so does Don, the same half step. The music drops out; the silence is the joke |
| **Reaction** | MCU: Sofía sees her CEO saved by his mother |

**Why it matters:** the Client gets his fight before Mom ends it. Her look ends the argument, not the joke. It costs Don more than it saves him: he's shrunk in front of Sofía. Mom is silent, with no "DONNIE!", as canon asks.

### Beat 6: The reversal and the loop (33–43 s)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Action** | MS: the Client takes his phone and walks out past Mom. No blueprint |
| **Action** | MS, the corridor: his phone pings. He stops |
| **Insert** | INS, CU on the phone: **100% COMPLETE**, and under it, tiny: *approved interface changes* |
| **Reaction** | MCU: the Client reads it. Nothing moves except his jaw |
| **Last frame** | The big screen, close: green **0 RESULTS**, the blue strips in the bin beside it. The cut back to frame 0 (the same screen, empty) closes the loop |
| **Sound** | The phone ping, then the hum returns |

**The loop:** the image returns to the start; the story has moved on. The screen that was empty at frame 0 now shows a green zero, and he has paid for a guarantee.

## Gates

**G0 series check:**
- what Don wants: admiration, with Sofía watching;
- who takes it: the Client's challenge, then Mom saving him;
- the ironic surface: a colour fix and a Friday guarantee;
- the iconic image: the green 0 RESULTS beside the blue strips;
- someone who matters sees him fail: the Client, Sofía and Mom.

**G1 protect list:**
1. "Guaranteed by Friday", a real sale.
2. The red zero by about 12 s.
3. The green zero: Sofía's words, Don's literal mind, "Approved."
4. "Did you find my blueprint?" / "We fixed the red."
5. Mom's look, and both men stepping back.

**G3 composition:**
- faces just under the title bar;
- the shredder bin as the foreground layer in beats 1, 3 and 6;
- the screen text whole in frame or out of it;
- the price card and the phone text legible at 270 px wide.

## Risks

| Risk | Guard |
|---|---|
| The bin insert is missed | It's at 1 s and in the last frame, and the strips are the only blue in the film |
| "It's AI-powered" reads as a lie | Don delivers it with real pride, never a wink |
| Mom kills the comedy | She comes after line 10, says nothing, and the silence carries the beat |
| Six beats in 43 s is tight | If something must go, cut line 4 (the features dodge) first; the watch check carries it |
| Five characters in one room | Never more than three speak in a beat; Mom and the robot each have one moment |
| Sofía's face is too subtle at phone size | A CU, four clearly different expressions, about half a second each |
