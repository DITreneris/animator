## Logline

The garage owner needs his blueprint for a manufacturer on Friday. Don sells him Premium, "Guaranteed by Friday", turns to Sofía for applause and finds her smiling at the Client. The search returns 0 RESULTS. Sofía says to present it as a clean result; Don has the zero turned green. "Did you find my blueprint?" "We fixed the red." The Client leans in, and Mom appears: one look, and both men step back. He leaves, and his phone says 100% COMPLETE. The audience knows the blueprint is confetti in the bin.

## The joke in one line

**One green zero hurts three people three ways:**
- Don is genuinely proud of it.
- Sofía is embarrassed by it, because it came from her words.
- The Client can't believe it.

## Where we are in the story

- **Episode 9 must show** the Client handing the blueprint over ("We'll take care of it.") before Don sweeps it into the shredder. The green **ONBOARDING COMPLETE** stamp is then his false assurance. Without the handover, this episode makes him look careless.
- **From episode 9:** the strips are confetti; the rest are in the shredder bin; one strip is in his pocket, unread.
- **World rule:** Find works; no customer data was ever connected.
- **Into episode 11:** the Client comes back furious, and Pam finds the strips in the bin.

## What carries forward (into episode 11)

- He paid for **Premium, guaranteed by Friday**. Friday will come.
- The dashboard and his inbox say **100% COMPLETE**.
- The strips are in the **shredder bin**, and one is in his pocket.
- **Mom has seen Don lose a client's respect.** A seed for Father and the investor thread.

## G1 protect list

1. **"Guaranteed by Friday"**: a real sale, sincerely made.
2. **The red zero by about 12 s.**
3. **The green zero:** Sofía's words, Don's literal mind, "Approved."
4. **"Did you find my blueprint?" / "We fixed the red."**
5. **Mom's look:** both men step back.

## Canon locked for this episode

- Don never knowingly lies about the blueprint. His literal mind is the joke.
- Sofía never asks for a fake. Her attempt to control how it looks backfires.
- The robot's report is true within its narrow approved scope, and never says the blueprint was found.
- The Client stays competent, with a credible reason to leave: Mom, and nothing left to argue with.
- The blueprint stays in the bin until episode 11.

## Risks for the concept stage

| Risk | Guard |
|---|---|
| Mom rescues the scene from its conflict | She arrives **after** "We fixed the red.", so the challenge has already landed. Her look ends the argument, not the joke |
