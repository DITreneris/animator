## The episode in one line

The Client needs his blueprint by Friday. Don, who wants Sofía's admiration, sells him Premium and then has the robot paint the empty result green. Everybody fixes the surface, never the problem: Sofía plasters the portrait, Don greens the zero, Pam tapes the plan, and Mom, where Don learned it, licks her thumb.

## The engine and the threads

- **Don** wants Sofía's admiration. Every move he makes is for her, and his eyes go to her after each one.
- **The Client** wants only his blueprint. Charm, plaster and green don't work on him.
- **Sofía** charms people as a cosmetic fix. She is the one who says the truth ("It found nothing.") and then denies her part (the tablet behind her back).
- **Mom** (ASSISTANT TO DON) believes she is helping. To her, Sofía is a hussy, the robot takes people's jobs, and Pam would make Don a good wife. Sweet to Pam and Don; the glare over the frames to Sofía and the robot. Her opinion of Sofía stays in her face and in two muttered words, never a slur.
- **Pam** doesn't know about Mom's dream. She just feels supported for the first time, and she works: the tape, the comment, the plan held up, the plan saved.
- **The robot** executes everything perfectly, including the wrong thing.
