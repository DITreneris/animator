## The principle

**Don wins the technical argument and loses the human one, in the same shot.**

## Beat by beat

### Beat 1: The zero (0–4 s)

- **Frame 0:** the wall screen, close. "blueprint" sits in the search bar, cursor blinking. His framed photograph is at the edge of the frame.
- **Screen:** **0 RESULTS**, red, by 2 s. The robot's paddle snaps up red. **The office bossa cuts dead.**
- **Pull back:** the Client in front of the screen, phone in hand: **FRI · BANK**.
- **CLIENT:** "Friday, the bank. I need my blueprint."
- **Background:** Pam at the back with blue strips and tape; Mom at her desk by the door.

### Beat 2: The sale (4–10 s)

- **Action:** Don's eyes drop to the phone, **BANK**, and come up shining. He turns the tablet: **UPGRADE · PREMIUM · GUARANTEED BY FRIDAY**.
- **DON:** "Garage eleven. Guaranteed by Friday."
- **Reaction:** the Client's face opens: someone understands what he needs. Behind them, Mom straightens, proud.
- **Thumb 1:** his oily thumb on the tick. A soft *ka-ching*.

### Beat 3: The zero, again (10–14 s)

- **Screen:** the robot searches again. **0 RESULTS**, red. The same flat tone.
- **Reaction:** the Client looks at the tablet in his hand, then at Don. His phone buzzes: **BANK**.
- **SOFÍA**, low, to Don, for the Client's sake: "It's the red. It looks bad."

### Beat 4: The fix (14–21 s)

- **DON**, lit up, to the robot: "Can you make the zero green?"
- **ROBOT:** "Interface change. Human approval required."
- **DON:** "Approved."
- **Thumbs 2 and 3, the match cut:** CU, Don's thumb presses APPROVED → CU, Pam's thumb presses tape onto a blue strip.
- **Screen:** the zero turns **green**; the paddle snaps up green; **the cheerful chime**. The phone buzzes BANK again, under it.

### Beat 5: The reveal (21–27 s)

One held composition, stacked in depth, no lines:
- **Front:** Don, turned to Sofía, waiting for applause.
- **Middle:** Sofía, smiling at the Client. She thinks the look is fixed.
- **Beyond her:** the Client leans to the green zero, looks for his plan, finds nothing, turns his head to Don, and holds.
- **Back:** Pam, a strip in her hand, looking at Don.

Then, within the hold: Sofía's smile dies. Don sees her face change and beams. Pam looks down and goes back to the tape.

### Beat 6: The confrontation, and Mom (27–34 s)

- **CLIENT**, slowly: "You said I'd never lose anything again."
- **DON**, proudly, at the screen: "We fixed the red."
- **Reaction:** Sofía, hearing her own words handed to the Client. Her fingers stop on the pearl.
- **Low angle:** the Client towers over Don under his own framed face. Don glances up at the photograph.
- **Mom** rises from her desk.
- **MOM:** "Young man."
- **Reaction:** the Client steps back, out of manners. Don steps back, by reflex. Sofía watches her CEO's mother rescue him.

### Beat 7: The consequence, and the button (34–42 s)

- **Action:** the Client leaves through the glass door. **The same chime:** a half-second flash of his phone, **100% COMPLETE**, *approved interface changes*.
- **The button:** Pam comes forward and holds the taped plan up to Don, bays 1–10 with a gap at 7. His eyes are on Sofía. Without looking he takes it and swings it toward the shredder slot. Pam's hand catches it and pulls it back to her chest. He never notices.
- **Final wide:** the green zero, his framed face, Pam holding the plan, Mom back at her desk.
- **The loop:** the robot clears the screen for the next search; "blueprint" sits in the bar, cursor blinking. That's frame 0.

## Gates

**G0 series check:**
- what Don wants: Sofía's admiration;
- who takes it: the Client's silence, then his mother;
- the ironic surface: a colour fix, a Friday guarantee, a trophy photograph;
- the iconic image: **the green zero beside the framed first client**;
- someone who matters sees him fail: Sofía, Pam and Mom.

**G1 protect list:**
1. The red zero by 2 s, and the second one after he pays.
2. "It's the red. It looks bad." → the green zero → "We fixed the red."
3. The held shot of four looks.
4. "Young man.", and two men stepping back for two different reasons.
5. The near-shred, and Pam pulling the plan back.

## Canon check

- Don believes every word; he never knowingly lies.
- Sofía states a fact about how it looks; the green zero is Don's.
- The robot's report is true within its approved scope.
- The Client buys for a reason and leaves for a reason.
- The plan stays with Pam, bay 7 missing, for episode 11.
