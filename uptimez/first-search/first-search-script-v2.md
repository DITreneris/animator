## Rules of staging

- **One master wide** (episode 9's camera) opens and closes the film; the cut back to frame 0 is invisible.
- **The portrait is a fuse.** It is over Don's head in every wide. Every buzz rattles it. It is always crooked at 8°.
- **Thumbs are inserts, close, square on:** the Client's oily thumb, Don's thumb, Pam's thumb. Nobody mentions them.
- **Don never looks at what he's handed** (the tablet back to Sofía, the plan towards the shredder). His eyes are on Sofía or on the customer.
- **The robot is dead centre in its singles,** and literal: "Interface change." is all it says.
- **Phone inserts are the Client's clock:** FRI · BANK, twice (1.4–2.1, 3.3); PREMIUM · PAID once; 100% COMPLETE once, in the doorway.
- **Reactions are faces, close,** one at a time; the reveal (5.1) is the exception: four faces in depth, one held shot.
- **Text** sits in the safe area; the screen's words are in the picture, not cards; no speech bubble covers a face.
- **It works on mute:** every line has a face or a screen to read it from.
