## Rules of staging

- **One master wide** (episode 9's camera) opens and closes the film; the cut back to frame 0 is invisible.
- **Frame 0 reads in three priorities:** (1) the robot's raised finger and the screen with "blueprint", upper left; (2) the portrait, large (at least 120 px wide at 540), crooked 8°, directly above (3) Don's bald head, centre. The Client stands in the open doorway at the right, mid-ground; Sofía half behind Don; Pam and Mom small at the back. Nothing else moves before 0.5 s.
- **Nothing the viewer must understand lives only in the "Thinks" column.** Thinks are acting notes. Every meaning is in a face, a hand, a screen or a line.
- **The portrait is a fuse:** over Don's head in every master wide, rattled by every buzz, always crooked at 8°.
- **Thumbs are inserts,** close and square on. Nobody mentions them.
- **Don never looks at what he's handed:** the tablet back to Sofía, the plan towards the shredder.
- **The robot is dead centre in its single,** and literal.
- **One dominant event per shot** in the last 8 seconds.
- **Text** stays in the safe area; the screen's words are in the picture; no bubble covers a face.

## The protect list (gate G1)

1. **The hook:** the robot's ENTER, the red zero at 0.7 s, the portrait on Don's head at 1.25 s; the second zero, the portrait swinging and holding.

## Gates

**G0 series check:** Don wants Sofía's admiration; the Client wants his blueprint by Friday. One mechanism, the series engine the audit names: **a character fixes the wrong problem, the robot executes it perfectly, the system celebrates, reality stays broken.** The iconic image is **the green zero beside the crooked framed first client.** The image loops (the robot retries, the portrait will fall again) while the story advances (he paid; Premium only means the zero returns until Friday; the report says 100% COMPLETE). Status moves: the Client from buyer to giant to a man stopped in a doorway; Don from bonked to saved by his mother.

**G2 shot list:** each beat has an action shot and a reaction shot with size and eyeline (tables above). Watch on the board: 5.1 (two reactions in one medium three-shot) and 6.1 (the baby photo legible at 270 px).

**G3 composition, to check on the board:** frame 0's three priorities; the portrait at least 120 px wide; RETRY UNTIL FRIDAY legible at 270 px in 8.2.

**G4:** the cold mute test on the storyboard before any animation, with one extra question: "What starts again at the end?"
