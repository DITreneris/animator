## The idea

He came back to collect what he was promised. The office sells him a Friday guarantee, finds nothing, and fixes the colour of nothing, **under his own framed face.** Behind them, the only person doing real work is taping his plan back together, and says nothing.

**One green zero hurts three people three ways:**
- Don is proud of it.
- Sofía is embarrassed by it, because it came from her words.
- The Client can't believe it.

**The dramatic irony now has a body:** we watch Pam rebuild the very thing the screen can't find.

## Gates

**G0 series check:**
- what Don wants: admiration, with Sofía watching;
- who takes it: the Client, with Don's own words, then Mom;
- the ironic surface: a colour fix, a Friday guarantee, a trophy photograph;
- the iconic image: **the green 0 RESULTS beside the framed first client**;
- someone who matters sees him fail: the Client, Sofía and Mom.

**G1 protect list:**
1. "Premium. Guaranteed by Friday.": a real sale.
2. The red zero by about 12 s, and Pam's hand stopping.
3. The green zero and the green paddle: Sofía's words, Don's literal mind, "Approved."
4. "You said I'd never lose anything again." / "We fixed the red.", under his photograph.
5. Mom's look; both men step back. The last frame: the green zero, his photo, the plan with a gap at 7.

**G3 composition:**
- episode 9's camera positions, so the room is recognised at once;
- the screen and the photograph whole in frame together;
- Pam's desk as the background layer;
- text legible at 270 px wide.

## Risks

| Risk | Guard |
|---|---|
| Pam's taping is missed | The insert at 1 s, the foreground shot in beat 3, and the last frame. Blue is still the only blue in the room |
| Pam's silence reads as passivity | One action, a stopped hand, eyes to the screen then to Don, then carrying on: a choice, as in episode 9 |
| The photograph is too small to read | Beat 5's low angle frames his face and the photo together; the last frame holds them side by side |
| Six people in one room | Never more than three speak in a beat; Pam, Mom and the robot each get one moment |
| The bank reason costs a line | It's inside line 1 ("Friday, the bank.") and on his phone |
| 11 lines in 45 s | Cut line 5, then line 3 |
