# First Search: shot list v2 (draft, 19 Nov). Fewer cuts; the camera moves in the room

**Why:** the full film (long pacing, 46.5 s) had 36 shots, about 1.3 s each. Too many hard cuts, and the story was hard to follow.

**Rule:** when the next beat happens in the same place with the same people, the camera moves (a push-in, a pull-back or a tilt) instead of cutting. Characters keep their places within a shot; only their poses and faces change. Inserts (a full-screen object) stay only where the text is the joke, or where we cut into a memory.

**Result:** 20 shots instead of 36, the same 46.5 s, the same lines, about 2.3 s per shot.
