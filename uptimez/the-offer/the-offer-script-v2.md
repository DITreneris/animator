# The Offer: the script, v2 (for review)

UptimeZ Inc. · episode 13 · *Civil War* 1 of 5 · 22 Nov. Second draft, after an outside script review (22 Nov); v1 is kept. Nothing is drawn or recorded yet. **About 54 s on paper, 38 shots (eight of them second drawings in the same frame), 13 spoken lines.** The recorded voices set the real length; episode 12 grew by about a quarter between script and animatic, which would put this near 65 s, inside the arc's 60–90 s.

## What changed against v1

- **The demand is visible:** the bodyguard's thumb hovers over **DELETE POST** under the one-star post (0.4).
- **The danger is real:** the car rocks on its stands over the man underneath, the sump a hand above his face, when he says "Off the car." (0.8–0.9).
- **The threat names a garage:** the loyal bodyguard puts his finger on GARAGE 7 on the plan, looks round at the real bay, and the paper starts to brown under the flame (1.1–1.2). The brown mark stays. Line: "Burn them. **Your son** loses his only customer."
- **The two bodyguards differ by what they do:** the first obeys the Client (off the car, the lamp); the second stays loyal (the lighter, the phone).
- **Father watches the cause:** after the first bodyguard gets off the car (0.10), Father has seen it; when the same man takes the lamp, a new close-up of Father watching is where he decides (2.3b). After "Without him." he weighs it in silence before "And?" (2.6a).
- **One readable fact per screen:** the contact card fills the phone (2.8); the robot's visor says only WITHOUT HIM, and the robot reads it out ("New meeting. Agenda: without him."); Don only asks "Without who?".
- **One ending:** the robot's slow head turn is the peak; then Don's smile dies, Pam turns her phone face down, Don calls Dad, and the garage.
- **Cut:** the plan insert (folded into the lighter shot), Father's second eyebrow, Don reading the agenda aloud, Sofía (saved for episode 14). The handshake and "Done." are one drawing.
- **Kept against the review:** the length target. The review asked for 50–55 s; the arc is 60–90 s, and the voices will take this there without padding.

## In one line

Father comes to the Client's garage to make him take down the one-star post. Everything he brings to frighten him, the Client turns into garage work, until Father asks him how he would run Don's company. "Without him." The Client's terms are Pam. Back at the office, the robot reads the new meeting out loud: AGENDA: WITHOUT HIM.
