# The Offer: the script, v1 (for review)

UptimeZ Inc. · episode 13 · *Civil War* 1 of 5 · 22 Nov. First draft from STORYLINE v3.3 (Act D). Nothing is drawn or recorded yet. **About 57 s on paper, 41 shots, 14 spoken lines.** The recorded voices will set the real length; episode 12 grew by about a quarter between script and animatic, which would put this at 65–70 s.

## In one line

Father comes to the Client's garage to make him take down the one-star post. Everything he brings to frighten him, the Client turns into garage work, until Father asks him how he would run Don's company. "Without him." The Client's terms are Pam. Back at the office, the robot reads the new meeting out loud: AGENDA: WITHOUT HIM.

## G1 Protect list

1. **The kingpin threatening a pair of legs.** The Client doesn't roll out until the flame.
2. **"Burn them. You lose your only customer."** The line that turns Father.
3. **PAM — finds things.** The contact card from episode 11, read on mute.
4. **The bodyguard holding the lamp.** The threat ends up as garage help, and it is the last frame.
5. **AGENDA: WITHOUT HIM.** The robot reads it out cheerfully, Don asks "Without who?", and the robot turns its head.

## Open, for Tomas

1. **Length.** About 57 s on paper; the voices will likely bring it to 65–70 s. Keep it as is, or add a beat (for example, the Client finishing the job and starting the car)?
2. **Father speaks to the Client.** Episode 12 had a rule: he speaks only to Don. Here he breaks it on purpose ("My son says his machine finds anything."). Keep it, or keep him silent and let a bodyguard's phone carry his questions as typed text?
3. **The burn threat** is a gold lighter held near the plan, with no line. Keep it mute, or give Father "It would be a shame if they burned." (the review's line)?
4. **Pam on the invite.** INVITED: PAM plants episode 16 (both camps bid for her), and Sofía left off plants 14. Keep both now, or save Pam's invitation for later?
5. **The last frame:** the bodyguard holding the lamp, plus the face-down phone on the toolbox. Both, or just the lamp?
