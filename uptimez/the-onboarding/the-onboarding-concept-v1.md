## The idea in one line

A man walks in and says "I'd like to sign up." It takes five people, three denied touches and seventy seconds to let him.

Everyone "helps" by adding a step. Only one person removes steps, and she adds one of her own.

| Who | The step they add | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Don** | A sales pitch, to a man who has already said yes | Sofía might watch |
| **Pam** | A form | It puts her next to Don |
| **HR robot** | A contact policy | It is the policy |
| **Sofía** | None: one tap and it is done. Then her CV | Her future |

## Who wants what

| Person | Shows | Hides |
|---|---|---|
| **The Client** | A polite man who has decided to buy | Nothing. He is the only normal person in the room, and the audience's eyes |
| **Don** | A CEO closing a deal | The deal does not matter to him. Sofía watching does |
| **Sofía** | Warm, efficient customer care | She is interviewing: he owns a business, so he is a future employer, partner or husband. She knows Don is watching and enjoys it |
| **Pam** | Process and paperwork | Every form is a reason to stand beside Don |
| **HR robot** | Perfect compliance | Nothing. Hands may not touch; a card may. It sees no contradiction |

**Why this is not last episode again.** In *The Best Candidate* Sofía was attracted to the macho. Here the Client is ordinary, so her flirting reads as ambition, and the CV is the proof.

## The running gag

Human contact is denied in red. Payment is approved in green.

| # | Beat | Contact | Robot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 02 | Don's handshake with the Client | "CONTACT NOT VERIFIED." |
| 2 | 05 | Pam's hand on Don's arm | "UNAUTHORIZED TOUCH." |
| — | 07 | The Client's card on the terminal | "CONTACT APPROVED." |
| 3 | 09 | Pam's high-five to Don | "CONTACT DENIED." |

**The rule that keeps it consistent:** objects may pass between people, hands may not. Sofía never touches the Client. She hands him a tablet, a pen, the welcome pack. She is the only one who has worked out how the office runs.

## The Client: brief

- **Who:** runs a small business with his own hands. Polite, patient, a little out of his depth with software, and already decided: he was told he needs "an AI".
- **Why he buys after "0 results":** his current system also finds nothing, only slower. "Mine takes a minute."
- **In this episode:** hopeful, puzzled, pleased. **In the next:** the same man, unable to find anything. The sheet carries faces for both.
- **Look:** must read at once as different from Don (bald, vest), the macho (broad, handsome) and the small one (spiky, glasses). Three directions to draw as options:
  1. **The baker:** round, soft, cardigan with flour on the cuff.
  2. **The garage owner:** flat cap, work jacket, big careful hands.
  3. **The one-man firm:** short-sleeved shirt, pens in the pocket, a tote bag of paper invoices.
- **Voice:** three auditions chosen by measured pitch, away from Don, the macho and the small one. Warm and unhurried.

## Canon to keep

- It is **Sofía**. She is in her office look; new earrings for a client day are yours to choose.
- **Don** wears the fleece vest and lanyard, not a tie. The ketchup stain is there and nobody mentions it.
- **Pam** has her reading glasses on the chain. Her cup is new; it can be cacao if you want it to become hers.
- The **robot** is the HR robot, with its capitals and its one-line verdicts.
- The office never learns the Client's name. His VISITOR badge is the same kind Don crossed out for himself last episode.

## How it meets our rules

| Rule | In this concept |
|---|---|
| One joke | A simple task takes everyone. The contact gag and Don's unreturned attention both serve it |
| Reads muted | Every beat has one action a silent viewer can name (last column above) |
| Text to read | Five full-screen inserts: the two searches, the payment, the CV corner, the end card. The robot's rulings are a red or green symbol plus its voice, not words on a wall |
| One speaker per shot | Each line gets a single with clear wall beside the face, as in animation v3. Group shots are for looks, not lines |
| Characters large | Five people never share one vertical frame. The chain of looks is cut as singles, all facing the same way |
| The kit | No low angles, over-the-shoulder shots or reflections. Don checks his look in his phone, not in glass |
| Loop | Door shut at the end, door opening at the start |

## Length, counted

Measured on *The Best Candidate*: 1.45 s per shot and 1.36 s per line.

- 22 lines is about 30 s of speech.
- About 48 shots is about 70 s.
- Five inserts and the two-second silence are inside that.

Expect the first cut near 75 s. **First cuts if it runs long:** "So… can I sign?", one reaction in beat 01, the second half of the celebration, "I'll close!".

## Risks

- **Sofía's flirt reads as a crush.** The CV has to be seen clearly, and her look at Don has to land.
- **Two door loops in a row.** *The Best Candidate* also looped on a door.
- **Confetti and bubbles.** Lines spoken under falling confetti need the bubble checks run on every drawing.
- **A new set.** Reception with a glass door, a demo desk and one monitor, with the signs hung high enough to leave wall for bubbles.
