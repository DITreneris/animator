## The idea in one line

A man with ten garages walks in with his best idea under his arm and says "I'd like to sign up." The office takes his dollar, and turns the idea into confetti to celebrate.

**The engine:** a simple task takes the whole office. **The fuel:** everyone is watching the wrong person, so nobody is watching the blueprint.

## Why this ending is stronger

The audit scored the ending 8/10 on v1 and 6/10 on v3. v3 had five things competing in the last 18 seconds, and its last laugh was a repeat with lower stakes. In v4 they are one thing:

| v3 | v4 |
|---|---|
| The celebration, then separately a failed search, then a fob | The celebration **is** the failure: the confetti is his blueprint |
| A receipt we never saw goes missing | A blueprint we watched him carry in goes missing |
| Text to read at the end | No text. It is the only blue paper in the film |
| A shrug | A face that starts to understand, and a door that shuts on it |
| Sets up the next episode by hinting | Hands the next episode its reason |

The loop then does one more thing: the door opens and he walks in again, hopeful, with the blueprint whole under his arm.

## The Client: brief

- **Who:** owns ten garages across the city and built them himself. Polite, patient, successful. He designs things, and loses the drawings.
- **Why he is here:** "I keep losing this." **Why he buys after "0 results":** his system also finds nothing, only slower.
- **Look:** a clean work jacket with his garages' patch, a rag in the back pocket, big hands with oil in the creases, a good watch, a heavy ring of keys with his car's fob. Mid-forties, solid. Nothing like Don, the macho or the small one.
- **Props:** the blue roll (beats 01–03), the welcome pack in its place (beats 06–10).
- **His faces:** friendly → polite confusion → tired acceptance → relief → the look at the door. Plus the angry ones for the next episode.
- **The smell:** acting only. Don's nose wrinkles; Sofía takes one breath.
- **Voice:** warm, low, unhurried. Three auditions by measured pitch.
- **Name:** nobody asks. His badge says VISITOR.

## Sofía: look and arc

- **Pearls:** drop earrings that swing, one strand over the dark collar, a bracelet that identifies her hand in the tap and CV inserts.
- **Four gestures, four jobs:** the smile (attraction), the necklace (interest), the CV (action), the tablet (obsession).
- **She is never cruel to Don.** She simply stops seeing him. She also never sees the shredder, because she is looking at the Client.
- **Her flirt line stays professional.** It is funnier when office language hides something that is not.

## Canon to keep

- **Don:** fleece vest and lanyard, the ketchup stain there and unmentioned.
- **Pam:** reading glasses on the chain; professional throughout. Hopeful → rejected → hopeful → rejected.
- **Robot:** the HR robot. No emotion, no apology. The celebration and its cannon are the function it already had; it gains no new one.
- **The Client is not a fool.** He accepts a metric without question, which is different.

## Risks

- **The shredder must be clear.** It needs its own insert in beat 03 and a clear bin that stays in view. If a viewer misses it, the ending is only blue confetti.
- **The strip at phone size.** Half a gear in white on blue, large in the palm.
- **Beat 09 is one long hold with four actions.** It is the centre of the film and the hardest shot to stage.
- **Blue confetti over speech bubbles.** The bubble checks run on every drawing.
- **Two crush episodes in a row.**
- **A new set:** glass door, demo desk, monitor, shredder with a clear bin, the cannon.
