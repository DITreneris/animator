## The idea in one line

A man with ten garages walks in with his best idea under his arm and says "I'd like to sign up." The office takes his dollar, and turns the idea into confetti to celebrate.

**The engine:** a simple task takes the whole office. **The fuel:** everyone is watching the wrong person, so nobody is watching the blueprint.

## Why this ending is stronger

The audit scored the ending 8/10 on v1 and 6/10 on v3. v3 had five things competing in the last 18 seconds, and its last laugh was a repeat with lower stakes. In v4 they are one thing:

| v3 | v4 |
|---|---|
| The celebration, then separately a failed search, then a fob | The celebration **is** the failure: the confetti is his blueprint |
| A receipt we never saw goes missing | A blueprint we watched him carry in goes missing |
| Text to read at the end | No text. It is the only blue paper in the film |
| A shrug | A face that starts to understand, and a door that shuts on it |
| Sets up the next episode by hinting | Hands the next episode its reason |

The loop then does one more thing: the door opens and he walks in again, hopeful, with the blueprint whole under his arm.

## The Client: brief

- **Who:** owns ten garages across the city and built them himself. Polite, patient, successful. He designs things, and loses the drawings.
- **Why he is here:** "I keep losing this." **Why he buys after "0 results":** his system also finds nothing, only slower.
- **"I live alone."** A fact, said the way he would give his postcode. He does not hear the question behind the question, and never catches up.
- **Look (sheet v3):** a good olive field jacket worn open over a white open-necked shirt, a belt, a good watch, the garages' gear as a small collar pin, one car fob at his belt, big hands with oil in the creases. Around fifty, grey at the temples, solid. An owner, not a mechanic. Nothing like Don, the macho or the small one.
- **Props:** the blue roll (beats 01–03), the welcome pack in its place (beats 06–10).
- **His faces (sheet v3):** confident → warm → puzzled → suspicious → shock → exhausted → relief → the look at the door. Plus the angry ones for the next episode.
- **The smell:** acting only. Don's nose wrinkles; Sofía takes one breath.
- **Voice:** B from the audition: gruff, a big chest, about 124 Hz, five semitones above Don.
- **Name:** nobody asks. His badge says VISITOR.

## Sofía: look and arc

- **In control.** With the macho she was flustered; that was her one crack in the series. With the Client she is choosing. No blush, no dropped pen.
- **Pearls for gold:** a single drop on each ear that swings, one short strand lying on the dark lapels, a one-row bracelet on her screen-left wrist. The bracelet identifies her hand in the tap and CV inserts.
- **The tablet:** at her hip with the CV clipped to its back from the first beat; held out with one green button for "Tap here."; at the end, the screen with his thumbprint.
- **Four gestures, four jobs:** the smile (attraction), the look and the pearls on "Oh..." (she finds out), the CV (she acts), the tablet (her trophy).
- **The look:** eyes down, six drawings held, eyes up. Half lids, her raised eyebrow, a small closed smile. A close single; in a two-shot her eyes are too small.
- **"Oh...":** low, drawn out, falling. A built take, chosen by pitch and then by your ear.
- **Don:** one knowing glance, once, straight after "Oh...". She is never cruel to him; after that he is simply no longer in the room. She also never sees the shredder, because she is looking at the Client.
- **One personal question, and only one.** "Does your missus love your garages?" is small talk on the surface, and sweet. After it she goes back to office language, which is now funnier: "Everything you need is in there."

## Canon to keep

- **Don:** fleece vest and lanyard, the ketchup stain there and unmentioned.
- **Pam:** reading glasses on the chain; professional throughout. Hopeful → rejected → hopeful → rejected.
- **Robot:** the HR robot. No emotion, no apology. The celebration and its cannon are the function it already had; it gains no new one.
- **The Client is not a fool.** He accepts a metric without question, which is different.

## Risks

- **The shredder must be clear.** It needs its own insert in beat 03 and a clear bin that stays in view. If a viewer misses it, the ending is only blue confetti.
- **The strip at phone size.** Half a gear in white on blue, large in the palm.
- **Beat 09 is one long hold with four actions.** It is the centre of the film and the hardest shot to stage.
- **Blue confetti over speech bubbles.** The bubble checks run on every drawing.
- **Two crush episodes in a row.**
- **Beat 03 must read muted.** The words carry the joke; without sound it has to be her eyes going down him and up, and Don's face seeing it.
- **"Oh..." is one syllable.** The plain takes are 0.4 to 0.5 s and flat. It needs its own built take: drawn out, falling, chosen by pitch and then by your ear.
- **Beat 03 now holds five lines and the shredder insert.** It is the busiest beat; the insert must still get its own clear second.
- **A new set:** glass door, demo desk, monitor, shredder with a clear bin, the cannon.
