**Target length:** about 70 seconds, ten beats, 19 lines. Real timing comes from the voice takes.

## The idea in one line

A man with ten garages brings in the one drawing he keeps losing. Don promises him he will never lose anything again, and means it. With the same sweep of his arm, the drawing goes into the shredder.

**Two wants that collide:** Don wants to impress the Client, and wants Sofía to see it. Sofía wants out from under Don, and the Client is her way out.

**One mechanism:** everyone is watching the person they want something from, so nobody watches the blueprint. Except Pam, who says nothing.

**One image:** a green stamp, ONBOARDING COMPLETE, over blue confetti that used to be his drawing.

## The characters in this episode

### Don
- **A real win.** The pitch is good and the Client believes it. Nothing on screen undercuts him while he says it, except what his arm is doing.
- **He never knowingly lies.** He does not look at the desk. That is the Don of every episode.
- **Under pressure, a grander title:** "Founder. And CEO."
- **"Approved."** is said to the Client's condition without a question. The same word carries episode 10.
- **After the sale** his first move is the vest and a look to Sofía.

### Sofía
- **In control.** No blush, no dropped pen. With the macho she lost her cool; here she is choosing.
- **What she cannot control is him.** Her opening gets a practical question back. Later he respects her for one tap that was Pam's work.
- **One real smile,** in beat 06, for half a second, and she catches it. The smile at the door is the practised one.
- **The CV is an application:** her portrait, her number circled, no heart. It leaves both routes open.
- **One personal question, and only one.** After it she speaks office language: "Everything you need is in there."
- **Pearls for gold,** the tablet, and the thumbprint as her trophy, as on sheet v1.

### Pam
- **She has no lines.** Her part is what she does not say.
- **Competence first.** She is typing before anyone else has moved, and the form is done before anyone asks.
- **Strategic silence, out of love.** She is the only one who sees the shredder. She lets Don have his moment.
- **Her contradiction in one image:** his hand is finally free, and she is on the floor fixing it.
- **She never lets the customer down.** The strips in her fist are the start of episode 11.
- **Unrequited, once.** No hand on his arm, no high-five. One look at his raised hand.

### The robot
- **Every statement is true:** the contact was not verified; the payment was approved; there is a first customer; the onboarding is complete.
- **Within its permissions:** it blocks, reports, celebrates and stamps. It does not approve the pilot or the price; Don does.
- **Worse by procedure:** the celebration is required, the bin is what is to hand.
- **A report and a stamp.** No emotion, no apology.

### The Client
- **Not an idiot.** He asks one practical question, sets one testable condition, and gives it one month.
- **He starts out believing confident professionals deliver.** Don earns that belief honestly, for now.
- **"I live alone."** A fact, said the way he would give his postcode.
- **"That worked."** is respect for a result. He does not know whose result it was.
- **His watch** is checked twice: before the condition, and at the door.
- **Look and voice:** sheet v3; voice B.

## Risks

- **The shredder must be clear.** Its own insert, and a clear bin that stays in view. If a viewer misses it, the ending is only blue confetti.
- **Pam's silence must read.** A close single: eyes on the bin, mouth open, eyes to Don, mouth shut. Without it she is just absent.
- **The Client must not look foolish at the door.** He is puzzled by a scrap of paper; he is not shown a clue and ignoring it. One strip, few lines on it.
- **Beat 03 holds five lines.** It is the busiest beat and carries Sofía's whole turn.
- **"Oh..."** still needs its own built take: low, drawn out, falling.
- **Beat 09 is one long hold with four actions.**
- **The season plan changes** if this is approved: episodes 10 and 11 search for and repair the blueprint.
- **Voice B is `am_fenrir`,** which was Don's voice in the prequel shorts. It is four semitones lower here; confirm by ear that it does not recall him.
- **A new set:** glass door, desk, shredder with a clear bin, cannon, card terminal.
