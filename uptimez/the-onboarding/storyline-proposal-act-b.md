# Proposal: Act B of Season 1, around the blueprint

For `STORYLINE.md` v3. A proposal only: nothing in the repo's canon files has been changed.

## The change in one line

The pilot's testable condition becomes **"find my blueprint"** instead of "find a van's repair history". One prop carries episodes 9, 10 and 11: it is shredded, it cannot be found, and Pam tapes it back together.

## Why

- **The Client handed it over.** "Find my blueprint" is a fair demand on a company that said it would take care of it.
- **The viewer has seen it destroyed.** Episode 10's 0 RESULTS becomes dramatic irony instead of a first disappointment.
- **It reads on mute.** A blue roll, blue strips, blue confetti, a taped blue sheet. A van's repair history is text.
- **Pam's hidden work becomes visible.** Taping strips is something you can watch; phoning garages is something you are told.
- **It adds no lore about Find.** Find returns 0 RESULTS for the canon reason: the blueprint was never connected to it. It was never connected because it was confetti.

## What it costs

- The ten garages matter less to the search. Pam can still phone a garage in episode 11 for the piece that is missing.
- Episode 9 no longer ends with Don's signature on Sofía's application. That beat moves to episode 10.
- It is untested. Show episode 9 to cold viewers before locking 10 and 11.
