# The Onboarding — script v4 (line by line, with the shot list)

UptimeZ Inc. · episode 9 · from concept v9 · for review · nothing is animated from this yet

v2 takes in the grill of 17 Nov: Pam's silence is a finger raised and lowered; CAUGHT is lips flat and eyes to Don; the pearl is an insert and she rolls it; Don stays small and tight in his office look; Pam is as in The Best Candidate and collects the strips in her bare hands. The acting is drawn on `ob_cast_v2.png`.

v3 takes in the acting audit: Sofía's hand freezes mid-roll when Don notices, and her smile goes before her eyes turn; Don, caught, becomes more professional instead of angry; the Client hesitates before he lets go of the roll, and pockets the strip with his eyes already back on the room. v1 and v2 are kept.

v4 takes four things from the comedy audit of the set sheet: the robot stops the hand and leans toward the card; Don pumps a fist at one dollar; the cannon catches Don, who keeps beaming; in the last frame the Client is walking away, not facing the glass. It does not take the rest: the Client is not shut out or humiliated, and does not find out. v3 is kept.

**Length:** about 78 seconds, 55 shots, 20 lines. Concept v9 said 72 s; the reaction chain in beats 03 and 06 needs the difference. Real timing comes from the voice takes.

**Staging:** the staff stand on the right and look left; the Client enters from the left. A line is always spoken in a single with clear wall for its bubble. A reaction always shares a frame with its cause.

## The protect list (gate G1)

1. **The handover and the loss are one action:** he gives it; "We'll take care of it."; Don sets it in the shredder without looking.
2. **Pam sees it and stays silent:** eyes on the bin, a finger raised to interrupt, eyes to Don, the finger lowered.
3. **"Does your missus love your garages?" / "I live alone." / "Oh..."**, her eyes, the pearl, and Don seeing it.
4. **The twin framing:** the handshake is blocked, the card is approved.
5. **The last frame:** the green stamp on the door, blue confetti behind it, Pam's fist full of strips.

## What the cold mute test changed

Two rounds, two fresh viewers each, frames only (results in the session report). These are now rules for this script:

| Finding | Rule here |
|---|---|
| In singles nobody knew whom she was looking at | Beat 03 opens on a shot with all three; her look gets an insert of what it is on |
| The fingers on the pearl read as pointing | The pearl is an insert, the hand comes up from below, and the pearl **moves**: she rolls it between finger and thumb |
| "She catches him" read as her speaking to him | Her mouth is closed in that shot, and **Don looks away** |
| The last Sofía frame read as a pout at the Client | CAUGHT is lips pressed flat and eyes cut hard to Don; never a rounded mouth |
| The thumb had no owner | The Client's cuff and sleeve are in the insert |
| Nobody knew who Pam was | Pam is in the first wide shot, typing |
| Don's stain was taken for the story | In his reaction close-ups the frame line is above it |
