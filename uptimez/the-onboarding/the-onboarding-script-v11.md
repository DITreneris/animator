v2 takes in the grill of 17 Nov: Pam's silence is a finger raised and lowered; CAUGHT is lips flat and eyes to Don; the pearl is an insert and she rolls it; Don stays small and tight in his office look; Pam is as in The Best Candidate and collects the strips in her bare hands. v3 takes in the acting audit: Sofía's hand freezes mid-roll when Don notices, and her smile goes before her eyes turn; Don, caught, becomes more professional instead of angry; the Client hesitates before he lets go of the roll, and pockets the strip with his eyes already back on the room. v1 and v2 are kept.

v4 takes four things from the comedy audit of the set sheet: the robot stops the hand and leans toward the card; Don pumps a fist at one dollar; the cannon catches Don, who keeps beaming; in the last frame the Client is walking away, not facing the glass. It does not take the rest: the Client is not shut out or humiliated, and does not find out. v3 is kept.

v5 follows storyboard v2 (after the third mute test and the director's audit): her look lands on his face, not his hand; the Client is in her frame for the pearl; Don reacts alone, by going still, then covers; the drawing is shown open, with one large gear; Pam's finger shares a frame with Don; the robot is in the first shot; Pam starts down in 8.5. Still 55 shots; cuts can come later. v4 is kept.

v6 takes in the decisions of 17 Nov after the animatic: his drawing is the **plan of his ten garages** (ten numbered bays, white on blue), not a gear, so the strip in his palm carries a bay and a number; Don, caught, takes hold of his vest and pulls it straight; Pam is on her knees with one hand on a strip, and looks up at Don, who does not see her; "Oh..." is take A. The wall has an empty frame, OUR FIRST CLIENT, which nobody mentions. v5 is kept.

v7 takes in the director's notes on animation v5 (17 Nov) and his four answers: **Don's jealousy is one shot** (3.9: the sales smile, he goes still, his eyes go down, he pulls his vest straight and smiles too wide; 3.10 and 3.11 are cut, her "caught" look stays in 6.7); **Pam's silence is one shot** (5.1: finger up, eyes from the bin to Don behind her, finger down; 5.2 and 5.3 are cut); **no emoji pops**; **the product is named on the screens** (UPTIMEZ FIND · 1-MONTH PILOT on the tablet and on the payment); "Oh..." starts on its vowel (the breath before it was heard as "so"); name badges are whole or out of frame; bubble tails end beside the mouth.

v8 takes in the director's notes on animation v6 (17 Nov) and his four answers: **Pam has one remark**, in the held shot of beat 09, to nobody: "Some shit, others scoop." (her silence in beat 05 is unchanged; this is her only line); **the CV carries a telephone handset drawn in lipstick** beside the circled number; **the ending is trimmed**, a little off every hold from 8.4 to the last frame, no shot cut.

v9 is the final polish (17 Nov), nothing in the story changes: in 3.1 **his arms hold the plan and the roll** (they hung at his sides while it floated); in 3.3 and 3.5 the roll is **under his arm** (it was nowhere); **office music**: the ceiling-speaker bossa of The Best Candidate plays low under the film from the door, the cannon cuts it dead, so Don's shout and Pam's line are in silence, and it comes back with the stamp on the glass.

v10 takes in the cold mute test of animation v8 (two fresh viewers: three beats read whole, the same half of the other two missed by both) and the director's idea for the frame. Nothing is longer: **3.9 is a two-shot** (her hand turning the pearl in front, Don behind it, seeing it); **5.1 has the shredder in it**, its bin full of blue strips, beside Don: her eyes are on it, then on him; **8.5: she goes down in three drawings**, not in a cut; **the last shot: OUR FIRST CLIENT is filled** with his photograph, the face he made when the robot stopped his hand (the frame is empty in every shot before it, and empty again when the film loops).

v11 changes sound only (17 Nov, animation v10 and v11). **Pam's line is recorded again**: speech recognition on the final mix heard the first take as "Some shit other scooper"; the words are the same. **The office music is new**: the bossa was "a bit annoying" (too loud, never changing, a shaker all the way), and of three candidates the director chose A by ear: sparse vibes, one soft rolled chord a bar and a quiet bass note, no percussion, slower and 5 dB lower. It ducks a quarter of a second before each line, the cannon still cuts it dead, and it returns with the stamp. The film is 74.3 seconds.

## The protect list (gate G1)

1. **The handover and the loss are one action:** he gives it; "We'll take care of it."; Don sets it in the shredder without looking.
2. **Pam sees it and stays silent:** eyes on the bin, a finger raised to interrupt, eyes to Don, the finger lowered.
3. **"Does your missus love your garages?" / "I live alone." / "Oh..."**, her eyes, the pearl, and Don seeing it.
4. **The twin framing:** the handshake is blocked, the card is approved.
5. **The last frame:** the green stamp on the door, blue confetti behind it, Pam's fist full of strips.
