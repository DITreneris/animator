# Office Noir 4 — script v1 (line by line, with the shot list)

UptimeZ Inc. · Valentine's special · Pam's dream · from concept v2 and Tomas's picks of 23 Nov · for review · nothing is drawn or recorded from this yet

**Title:** *Office Noir: Seven Lives*. **On the marquee:** PAM-CAT. **"Only for you"** is on the sign only, never in her voice. **Calendar:** outside the season's count, a Valentine's special (14 February), as *Office Noir 2* was the New Year's.

**Length:** about 64 seconds by my estimate, 33 set-ups, 4 lines (three of narration, one spoken). Real timing comes from the voice takes.

## What changed from concept v2

| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| **The film opens on the aftermath, not on Pam asleep.** Frame 0: Pam-Cat already in her hero pose on the wet street, three pale-gloved shadows flat around her, Sofía walking towards her on her phone. At 0:01 Sofía steps over a shadow's glove and round Pam without looking up | Concept v2 said the joke landed at 0:01, but its own timetable put the step-round at about 0:06, after a second of Pam asleep and a fight. | **The shadows lead her to the car.** After the step-round they get up and run to a waiting sedan; she follows them | One chain of cause: the shadows → the car → the crossing → the casino. Nothing in the dream happens without the shot before it |
| **The sedan is parked outside the casino** | It tells the viewer how she found the place |
| **Dad's hand from the window is ringed**, as in *Daddy's Home* (ringed hand, cuff, chin, never the full face until the booth) | One design for Father across the series |

| **Valentine's on mute:** a neon heart in the florist's window in frame 0, a heart on the marquee, FEB 14 circled on the desk calendar | The setting has to say the occasion in the first frame (lesson 40). Sofía's mug stays plain, so nothing reads as romance |

## The protect list (gate G1)

1. **Three refusals, each worse:** walked round (1.2), walked over (3.5), turned off (6.4). Each is a body doing something, and each is followed by at least half a second of Pam's face.
2. **The hit-and-shrug, and Don wiping his bumper.** She is fine before the line lands; he checks the car, never her.
3. **The spotlight turned away** (6.4–6.5): one spotlight, the envelope held high, a pale glove turning the lamp to the cards, Pam left in the dark with her arm up. This is the shot and the cover.
4. **The deflation:** the dream's envelope wakes up as an internal-mail envelope holding the coffee rota, PAM on all seven lines. Same arm, same hold, an ordinary thing.
5. **"Only for you":** the marquee's advertising line in the dream, Sofía handing over a coffee awake. The first thanks Pam gets, for the wrong thing.

## Rules of staging

- **Her face carries every joke.** Half mask and cat ears only; eyes, brows and mouth readable at phone size (lesson 163). After every refusal: the pose holds, the eyes follow, the pose sags, she straightens her coat. That is her take (lesson 21), and the cut never comes on the gag's own frame (lesson 23).
- **Nobody in the dream ever looks at her.** Every eyeline in the dream goes past her: to a phone, a bumper, a bill on the road, a map, the cards. Hers go to them.
- **The pale gloves are the tell.** Every shadow has them: on the street, at the car, in the gutter, dealing cards, on the spotlight.
- **The dream is black and white,** lit with the series' noir kit (blinds, key light, vignette) in every location. Colour in the dream: the neon (heart, marquee) and the envelope's warm tone. Awake, the office is in colour and the rota envelope is the same warm tone.
- **Screen direction:** the chase runs left to right, street to crossing to casino. The sedan always drives off screen right.
- **Dead zones (G3):** faces out of y > 720 and out of the right-hand rail. When Pam lies in the road (3.2–3.5) the camera is low and the road line sits high, about y 640 at 540×960, so her face stays above the captions. The marquee and the rota insert sit in the top band, under the title bar.
- **Sizes:** series size for everyone (lesson 164); Father as in *Daddy's Home*.

## Voices and sound

- **Pam's narration:** her British voice from the audition, lower and drier than her office voice. Three lines, each over a picture that undercuts it. The voice stops at the marquee and never comes back.
- **Sofía:** `af_bella`, warm and offhand: "Only for you."
- **No other line.** Don's whistle, the Client's two taps, the ringed hand, the clink of the mug.
- **Music:** the jazz-noir cue from the first two films through the dream, cut dead on 6.3 (the silence of the waiting is the joke, lesson 41). A thin room tone and the clink when she wakes. Rain under 8.2 into the seam.

## Open questions

1. **Dad's face at the booth.** *Daddy's Home* hides it until he walks in. If *Office Noir 4* is released after episode 12, his face is already known and can be shown; if before, I'd keep him as cuff, ring and chin at the booth too.
2. **The map's Z.** The big Z is the only place UptimeZ is named in the dream. It has to read at 270 px; if it doesn't, the map goes and the scheme is only the envelope.
3. **Length.** If it runs long, the first cut is 4.2 (the marquee insert; 4.1 can carry the sign) and then 5.2 (the map insert).
