## The protect list (gate G1)

1. **Four refusals, each worse:** walked round (1.2), walked over (3.5), turned off (6.4), posted (7.2). Each is a body doing something; the first three are followed by at least half a second of Pam's face, the fourth by her waking to it.
2. **The hit-and-shrug, and Don wiping his bumper** and then straightening his vest. She is fine before the line lands; he checks the car, never her.
3. **The spotlight turned away** (6.4–6.5): the envelope held high, a pale glove turning the lamp to the cards, Pam in the dark with her arm up. The shot and the cover.
4. **The same arm, awake:** still up, and Sofía posts the rota into it. PAM on all seven lines.
5. **"Only for you":** big on the marquee in the dream; awake, said to her while she sleeps, about the coffee duty.

## Rules of staging

- **Her face carries every joke.** In the dream a narrow half mask and cat ears; eyes, brows and mouth readable at phone size (lesson 163). After each refusal: the pose holds, her eyes follow, the pose sags, she straightens her coat (a take, lesson 21). The cut never comes on the gag's own frame (lesson 23).
- **Nobody ever looks at her,** in the dream or awake. Every eyeline goes past her: to a phone, a bumper, a bill, a map, the cards, a mug. Sofía posts the envelope looking at her own phone.
- **The pale gloves are the tell.** Every shadow has them: on the street, at the car, in the road, dealing cards, on the spotlight.
- **No action under half a second** (lesson 158): the hit, the two taps, the glove on the lamp and the posting each get a pause before and time after.
- **The dream is black and white,** with the series' noir kit (blinds, key light, vignette) in every location. Colour in the dream: the neon (heart, marquee) and the envelope's warm tone. Awake, the office is in colour and the rota envelope has the same warm tone.
- **Screen direction:** the chase runs left to right. The sedan always leaves screen right.
- **Dead zones (G3):** faces out of y > 720 and off the right-hand rail. When Pam lies in the road the camera is low and the road line sits at about y 640 (540×960). The marquee and the rota insert sit in the top band under the title bar.
- **Sizes:** the series' size for everyone (lesson 164); Father as in *Daddy's Home*.

## Voices and sound

- **Pam's narration:** her British voice from the audition, lower and drier than her office voice. Three lines, each over a picture that undercuts it. It stops at the marquee and does not come back.
- **Sofía:** `af_bella`, offhand, said in passing: "Only for you." In a bubble, with the tail to her mouth as drawn in 7.2.
- **No other line.** Don's whistle, the two taps, the bump, the clink of the mug.
- **Music:** the jazz-noir cue of the first two films through the dream, cut dead on 6.3 (the silence of her waiting is the joke, lesson 41). Room tone and the clink awake. Rain under 8.2 into the seam.
