New (to add to `docs/lessons.md` after the next cold test confirms them):
- A hand that goes out of frame to "take" something reads as taking it from oneself; when the frame is widened, re-stage the reach toward the owner.
- A scorch on a dark curtain is a black blob; a burn needs something lighter seen through it and flames while it burns.
