## What it is

An AI office sitcom. Don, the CEO, finds out his father has a meeting without him. He tapes a line across the office floor and makes everyone pick a side. The HR robot answers "Both." Don then posts a team photo to prove he has a team. Across town, his father and the Client see the photo and recruit Sofía from it. The film ends with Don saying "Everyone's with me." while Sofía stands behind him holding a COO offer from his father. It loops: the last shot is the first.
