## Not taken (we disagree)

- **A tug of war of 0:00–0:08.** The joke lands by 0:01 and the blind tears at 0:02; 8 s would break the first-second rule.
- **"The tape is drawn up the wall."** That dashed line is the plan's own marking, not the animation; in the film the tape is on the floor only. The plan is unchanged there.
- **"DING unclear without the photo."** The photo already fills the screen in beat 8 (v3.1); the audit saw only the plan.
