# Pick a Side: concept v3

UptimeZ Inc. · episode 14 · *Civil War* 2 of 5 · 23 Nov · concept stage, for review. Nothing is built. v1 and v2 are kept as they are.

**Logline:** Don learns his father has a meeting "without him". He tapes a line through the office and makes everyone pick a side. Then he approves posting the team photo to the public, and the phone outside the door goes DING. By the end he says "Everyone's with me." while the picture shows the opposite.

**Format:** about 62 s, vertical. The front desk v4 (`src/fs_set4.py`) with blinds on UptimeZ's glass doors (13, 3.0), plus one shot of Father's club booth (12).

**When:** straight after episode 13, the same day, minutes later.

## What changed from v2

Decided by Tomas on 23 Nov (/grill-me), after a second outside audit ("Consider — revise before production").

| Change | Why (audit point) |

| **A neutral start:** in frame 0 there is no line. Everyone stands in the middle of the room; Don rips the tape straight through them, and they have to hop to one side or the other | In v2 they stood on Don's side before he asked (#6) |

## Risks

1. **Continuity of the floor:** the line's path, the spun throne, the torn blind, Pam's phone across the line, and who stands where in every shot. A top-down plan of the line on the front desk set before the board.
2. **The DING must read on mute:** the glow of the phone lifted to the silhouette's face, big enough on the glass.
3. **The robot ×3** costs about 5 s. Test the speed: the third return must be the fastest.
4. **Bodyguard 2 walks off in beat 11**, so the last frame has only the two planned differences.
5. **Length about 62 s.** First trims, if needed: beat 2 (fold into beat 1) and the knock line "Nobody's here."

## Test before production (from the audit)

A rough animatic of the blinds, the robot ×3, the public photo and the ending, shown to fresh cold viewers (G4). Ask: what did Don want, what went wrong, what made you laugh, and why did Sofía cross?
