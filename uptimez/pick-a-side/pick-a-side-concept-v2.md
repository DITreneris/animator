# Pick a Side: concept v2

UptimeZ Inc. · episode 14 · *Civil War* 2 of 5 · 23 Nov · concept stage, for review. Nothing is built. v1 is kept as it is.

**Logline:** Don learns his father has a meeting "without him". He tapes a line across the office floor and makes everyone pick a side. Then he lets the robot take a team photo and post it. Father sees it with the Client, and the Client spots the one heel on the wrong side of the line.

**Format:** about 60 s, vertical. The front desk v4 (`src/fs_set4.py`) with blinds on UptimeZ's glass doors (13, shot 3.0), plus one shot of Father's club booth (12).

**When:** straight after episode 13 ("Agenda: without him."), the same day, minutes later.

## What changed from v1

Decided by Tomas on 23 Nov (/grill-me), after an outside script coverage ("Consider") and the OK/fail triage.

| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| **The board meeting, quorum and minutes are gone** | Too sophisticated: office-law jokes, unreadable on mute. The quorum was also false, which broke the rule that the robot is technically true |
| **A tape line across the floor replaces the loyalty sheet** | The title becomes an action. Sides read on mute |
| **Sofía's precaution is a heel over the line** (the pencil is gone) | Physical; the Client can spot it in the photo |
| **The leak is a team photo:** "Team photo?" / "Approved." / FLASH / #TeamDon | Don's vanity (episode 10's post), mute-readable. Father learns from a post, as in 12 |
| **The first joke at 0:00–0:01 is physical: the blinds tug of war** | Series rule from now on: the 0:01 joke is an action, never a word or a sign |
| **Father is silent:** one shot, a gold pen, COO? | His second "Approved." read as a writer's callback (coverage); silence is more power |
| **The Client causes the offer:** he points at the heel, and Father acts on it | In v1 he only observed |
| **The bodyguard's silhouette is on the door glass from the photo on** | v1 had him teleport from the booth to the door |
| **Pam's phone lies face down on Father's side of the line** | v1's pocket glow couldn't be read; now her secret is a position on the floor |
| **Pam's remark stays but is new**, coming from her silent choice | Series rule (Tomas). The coverage's "cut it" was not taken; its note that she must visibly choose not to stop Don was |
| **The loop's difference: Sofía now stands on Father's side** | Don pays a price you can see: the meeting leaves him with less than he had |

## Risks

1. **Physical continuity:** the torn blind (from 0:02 until Don hangs it back at the end), the line's path, and who stands on which side in every shot. One top-down plan of the line on the front desk set before any board.

3. **Beat 12 is silent and carries the payoff.** About 1.5 s, the same cast on both sides of the cut.
4. **Bodyguard and booth reuse** must match 12's design exactly.
