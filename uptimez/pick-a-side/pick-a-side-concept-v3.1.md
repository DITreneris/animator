## What the animatic must prove (the audit's four checks + ours)

1. The same heel shows in the pose, in the photo and in the Client's zoom.
2. A cold viewer can say that Don approved publishing his own embarrassment.
3. The CV is handed over without Don plausibly noticing.
4. The last frame shows at once that Don's words contradict the picture.
5. (G4) Fresh viewers on mute tell back at least 4 of 5 beats; the 0:01 joke is read with no sound.
