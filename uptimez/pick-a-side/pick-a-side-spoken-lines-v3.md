## Things to check

1. **"Approved."** (10) is Don's runner (episodes 10, 11, 12): the same quick, unread delivery.
2. **"Donnie"** (5) is Mom's soft one, as in 12.
3. **Pam's remarks** (11 and 13), after "Some shit, others scoop." (9) · "Some post. Others tape." (10) · "Some post. Others find." (11) · "Some inspect. Others drink." (12); none in 13. **The formula:** the second half is Pam's own act ("Others notice"); "Others cross them" is the one exception, and it is earned by being literal (Sofía crosses on screen). Two in one episode is a one-off, for the war.
4. **"Pam. My general."**: Don is sincere. The joke is that he believes it; the competition for Pam (Don vs the Client's print on her in 4.2) is in the pictures, not in the words.
5. **Every speaker is in frame**, so every bubble gets a tail to a mouth drawn in that frame (the robot: its visor). No quoted cards.
6. **No bubble may cover a face:** 2.10 has two bubbles in 2.2 s ("And you?" and "Both."); stage them above each speaker, not between them.
7. **Short lines next to short lines** ("And you?" / "Both.", "Approved.", "Welcome."): the speech check gives each the line before as context (lesson 121); a miss there goes into the QA note by name, not into a re-record.
8. **Length:** the slots add up to about 25 s and the speech to about 14.5 s, so no line is rushed. If a take runs over its slot, the shot grows; the script's times are estimates until stage 2.
9. **Pronunciation:** nothing hard. "Donnie" as "DON-ee".
