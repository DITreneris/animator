# Pick a Side: concept v1

UptimeZ Inc. · episode 14 · *Civil War* 2 of 5 · 23 Nov · concept stage, for review. Nothing is built.

**Logline:** Don finds out his father has a meeting "without him", so he holds a secret loyalty meeting. His robot is the most transparent employee in the world: it logs the meeting as a board meeting, and Don approves the minutes without reading them. They go to the majority shareholder. Father reads them with the Client at his side. Before the episode is over, Sofía holds her CV again, with **COO?** written on it.

**Format:** about 65 s, vertical (the arc rule is 60–90 s). The front desk v4 (`src/fs_set4.py`) with the blinds down over UptimeZ's glass doors from episode 13 (3.0), plus one shot of Father's club booth from episode 12.

**When:** straight after episode 13 ("Agenda: without him."). Same day, minutes later. The date on the wall calendar is to be decided.

## The engine

| | |
|---|---|
| **One comic mechanism** | The most secret meeting in the world, held with the most transparent employee in the world |
| **Two competing desires** | Don wants secret loyalty. The robot wants every meeting logged correctly |
| **Who causes it** | **Don.** The robot asks for approval as procedure demands, and Don says "Approved." without reading. The robot never spies; every stamp is technically true |
| **Iconic image** | The door sign **SECRET MEETING — ALL WELCOME** beside Don whispering, blinds down |
| **Status move** | Don thinks he has secured his camp. In fact he has handed its members list to the other side, and the one person who signed in pencil has been noticed |
| **Carried consequence** | Father knows who is on Don's side; Sofía holds an offer; Pam still holds the invitation. **Two women each keep a secret from Don, and each knows the other's** |

## Canon check

| Rule | How this episode keeps it |
|---|---|
| The robot is technically true and acts only within its permissions | Board minutes going to shareholders is procedure. Sending company information out needs approval, and **Don gives it**. Every visor and stamp is true, including "Erasable." |
| Don never knowingly lies; under pressure, a grander title | "Head of Loyalty", then "Board meeting!" He believes the meeting is secret while he approves its publication |
| Sofía never fakes a record | Her pencil signature is real, only reversible. She keeps her options open; she doesn't forge anything |
| Pam: strategic silence, love for Don | She signs for real and hides the invitation. She read what he didn't, and lets him find out |
| Mom sparingly, knows Father is the investor | One line, one refusal: she knows a signature goes to Father |
| The Client is a player in every Civil War episode | One word and a watch check at Father's side, showing which camp he is in |
| Father is calm and never funny on purpose; he speaks only to Don, except to the Client (13) | One word, "Approved.", to nobody |
| Find never works | Find isn't used. No search in this episode |
| The image loops, the story advances | Frame 0 again, with the signed sheet on the wall; Father has the list, Sofía has the offer |
| The first joke by 0:01 | The sign behind the whisper, in frame 0 |

## Risks

1. **Father overexposure.** He has been in 12 and 13 and is here again. Keep him to **one shot and one word**; the Client in the booth carries the scene.
2. **The pencil must read on mute.** The robot's line covers the sound; on mute, the eraser close-up has to do it. Test it with cold viewers at the board.
3. **Too many paper props:** the sheet, the minutes, the CV and the invitation on a phone. Each needs its own shape and colour (the CV is white with red lipstick; the minutes are grey with a green stamp; the sheet is yellow).
4. **Beat 10 is the payoff, and it is silent.** The look needs about 1.5 s and the same cast on both sides of the cut.
5. **Who knows what.** After this episode, Father knows the camp, Sofía has an offer, and Pam has an invitation. Don knows nothing. Episode 15, *The Double Agent*, starts from exactly this.
