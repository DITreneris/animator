# Office Noir 2 — script v1 (line by line, with the shot list)

UptimeZ Inc. · New Year special · Sofía's dream · from concept v1 and the director's pass of 17 Nov · for review · nothing is drawn or recorded from this yet

**Working title:** *Office Noir: The Shadow of a Client*. The director's pass prefers *The Lady and the Blueprint*. Yours to choose.

**Length:** about 64 seconds by my estimate (the target is 60), 28 set-ups, 11 lines: six of narration, five spoken. Real timing comes from the voice takes; if it runs long, narration goes before pictures.

## Three things I took as decided, because you said "proceed"

Say so if any of them is wrong; each changes only a few shots.

1. **No gun is drawn.** Mom's shadow raises an arm with something dark in its hand, there is one pop and a flash, and the hat lifts. Your outline said she shoots him with a gun; the shadow still says that for one second, and the next frame says cork.
2. **The shadows are on the wall, not on glass.** With the trap, the Client is inside the room at Don's desk, so he cannot be a shadow on the door's glass. Don's desk lamp throws both shadows large on the office wall, and Sofía sees them from under the desk.
3. **The Bartender has no line,** and the Client has none when she wakes.

## The protect list (gate G1)

1. **Her narration says romance; the picture shows his hand going for a roll.** Umbrella, blueprint, party blower: the same shape, each time at her side or her chest.
2. **The napkin:** ten boxes and a question mark; her nail counts them; she turns it over and answers in lipstick, with a telephone.
3. **The trap:** she baits him with the blueprint, and when the keys jingle she saves herself. He is left at Don's desk, reaching for what has just gone.
4. **The bang:** two shadows on the wall, an arm up, one pop, the hat lifts. The same frame in colour is Mom with a champagne bottle.
5. **Awake she is holding a party blower where the blueprint was,** and it toots.

## Rules of staging

- **The dream is black and white with one red:** her lips, her nails, and what she writes. Nothing else has colour until she wakes.
- **The Shadow never has a face.** A hat brim, the collar of his jacket, the glint of the gear pin, the watch on his wrist, and his hand. What his hat brim points at is what he wants.
- **His hand always reaches for an object,** never for her. He is practical and a little lost, never a threat and never a flirt.
- **The narration is never about what we can see.** It stops for good when she goes under the desk.
- **He is always behind her, screen left;** his hand enters from the left.
- **The party frame is the shadow frame:** the same camera, the same positions, the same gesture, in colour.

## What it must not break

- **The blueprint stays lost.** It is whole only in her dream; awake, nobody learns anything, and nothing is carried into episode 10.
- **The Client is never hurt.** No weapon is drawn, there is no gunshot sound, and one cut later he is fine.
- **Mom shouts only in the dream,** which is Sofía's picture of her. Awake she is sweet, and she is the one holding the bottle.

## What the storyboard has to prove on mute

The director's ten checks, as questions for two cold viewers who see frames only:

1. Why is the man following her? (They should say: he is after something she carries, and she thinks he is after her.)
2. What is on the napkin, and what does she do with it?
3. Does she understand what he wants?
4. Why does she put the roll on the desk?
5. Why does she end up under the desk, and who is left standing?
6. Who comes in, and what do they think the man is doing?
7. What happens at the bang? And one frame later?
8. What is she holding when she wakes?
9. Was anyone hurt?
10. What is the last image, and does it lead back to the first?

## What has to be made before the storyboard

- **Sofía, noir:** her face, hair and proportions as they are; a fitted trench coat, the pearls, heels, the rolled umbrella. Red lips and nails.
- **The Shadow:** the Client's build, a hat, the collar, the pin and the watch catching the light, and his hand in several reaches.
- **The Bartender:** the nice macho in a white jacket; one glass, one eyebrow.
- **Mom:** as a shadow (curlers, coat, handbag, the raised arm), and as herself at the party with the bottle.
- **Sets:** the street (wet stones, one lamp, a shop window), the bar from one angle, the office at night with Don's lamp, the same office as a party.
- **Voices:** Sofía's narration in her lower voice from the first Office Noir; Mom's shout and her sweet line; the Client's five words.
