# Office Noir 2 — concept v4

UptimeZ Inc. · New Year special · Sofía's dream · for review · nothing is built from this yet

Written from concept v3, the director's-cut audit of 17 Nov, and your two notes on it: the dream is about her fear of being found out (she has no blueprint to give the Client, and she put her CV with her number into his welcome pack behind Don's back), and in the dream she sees Don take the napkin: she is exposed.

**Working title:** *Office Noir: The Shadow of Fate*.

**Target length:** 70–75 seconds. My estimate is 75 s in eight beats, with 5 lines of narration and 5 spoken. Real timing comes from the voice takes.

## The idea in one line

On New Year's Eve Sofía dreams she is the star the whole town wants, and spends the night running from the two men who could find her out.

**What she is hiding, from The Onboarding:** she told the Client "Everything you need is in there" and gave him her CV, her number circled, a telephone drawn in lipstick. Nobody has his blueprint. Don does not know about the CV.

**Wants that collide:** Sofía wants not to be found out, and to look like a star while she runs. The Shadow wants his blueprint. Detective Don wants a case he can close.

**One mechanism:** her narration plays the star; the picture shows a frightened woman leaving evidence behind her. The narration gets shorter as the fear gets bigger, and stops for good when Don catches her.

**One image:** two shadows on the office wall, a small one with its arm raised and a big one whose hat is lifting, and under the desk a red dress and a red mouth.

**Its twin, awake:** the same frame in colour. Mom holds a smoking champagne bottle; the Client's paper hat is in the air. He is fine.

## The protect list (gate G1)

1. **Her narration plays the star; the picture shows her running.** Twice: from the Shadow's hand, and from Don's eye.
2. **The napkin has two sides.** His question, ten bays; her answer, the lipstick telephone. She is caught by her answer, and nobody but her ever reads the question.
3. **The evidence she leaves:** the wrap, the white footprints, the number. Pam removes one, Don collects two.
4. **The trap and the bang:** the roll makes her a star again for ten seconds; then two shadows, an arm up, one pop. The same frame in colour is Mom with a bottle.
5. **Awake she is holding a party blower where the blueprint was,** and Don gives her a thumbs-up.

## Checked against the series

- **Sofía wants authority independent of Don, and never fakes a record.** The CV in the Client's pack is canon (episode 9). The dream is what that costs her at night. She fakes nothing: in the dream she finds a real roll, and awake she has none.
- **Sofía × Client: he values her judgement, not her performance, and that throws her.** His hand does not take her number.
- **Don approves instead of investigating.** One stamp, one side of the napkin, a drink.
- **Pam loves Don and keeps quiet.** She clears the trail that leads him to that dress, and says nothing when he asks with his eyes.
- **Mom sees through Sofía.** In the dream she is the shadow that arrives when Sofía is at Don's desk; the shout lands on the Client.
- **The robot needs approval for money.** It cannot keep a coin nobody approved.
- **The Client is never hurt, and the blueprint stays lost.** Awake nobody mentions a CV or a drawing, and nothing is carried into episode 10.

## What it must not become

- **A film you need episode 9 for.** Running, hiding and looking back must tell it alone. The lipstick telephone is the same drawing as on her CV for those who know.
- **A real exposure.** It happens in the dream. Awake, Don's thumbs-up is all we learn.
- **Dark.** The Shadow is never a threat: every time, his hand goes for a thing. Don catching her is a huge eye in a magnifying glass, then a man stealing a drink.
- **Powder as anything but face powder,** a likeness of a real person, a stage costume, new faces for the rich men, or a brand on the taxi: all as in v3.

## Three things I decided; say so if any is wrong

3. **Pam's line stays "Some dream. Others sweep."** The audit's "Missed a spot." is on the table if you want it.
