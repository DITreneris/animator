## What read without help

His hand going for the roll, and her face dropping. The sweeper erasing the trail as the detective arrives. The giant eye. The number ending up in his pocket. The cut from the two shadows to the party, with the hat becoming a paper hat. The roll becoming a party blower. The last window as the street of the first frame; both said she is choosing to go back into the dream.

## What this test cannot tell

Whether it is funny, whether the narration lands, and anything about timing or the mix.
