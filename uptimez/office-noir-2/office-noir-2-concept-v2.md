## The idea in one line

On New Year's Eve Sofía dreams she is the star every man in town wants. The only one she chooses wants his drawing back.

**Two wants that collide:** Sofía wants to be wanted, and to choose. The Shadow wants the plan of his ten garages, and thinks she has it.

**One mechanism:** her dream fills the city with people who want her, and her narration reads the Shadow the same way. The picture shows what his hand is reaching for.

**Why the new figures help it:** each one is a way of wanting her, or of not being impressed. The rich men offer money and she does not look. Detective Don follows her trail. The robot is the only one who gives something back. Pam is the only one who cleans up after her. The Shadow is the only one she turns round for.

**One image:** two shadows on the office wall, a small one with its arm raised and a big one whose hat is lifting, and under the desk a red dress and a red mouth.

**Its twin, awake:** the same frame in colour. Mom holds a smoking champagne bottle; the Client's paper hat is in the air. He is fine.

## What changed from script v1

| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| **The umbrella is gone; the first roll is her tube clutch** | You chose the dress alone. The Shadow still needs something rolled to reach for on the street. The clutch also holds the compact, so his reach is what spills the powder |

| **Her red nails become red gloves** | With long gloves the nails are covered. Red gloves keep her hands readable on the dark street, on the white napkin and over her mouth under the desk. White is the alternative; the cast sheet will show both |

| **Don, Pam and the robot are in the dream** | v1 left them out. Each gets one action and no line in the dream |
| **Awake, three things from the dream have a twin** | The powder is icing sugar, the blueprint is a party blower, Pam's broom is Pam's broom |

## The protect list (gate G1)

1. **Everyone in her dream wants her; the one she turns round for wants his drawing.** Narration says romance, his hand goes for a roll.
2. **The napkin:** ten boxes and a question mark; she counts them, turns it over and answers in lipstick.
3. **The powder trail:** spilled when he reaches, swept away by Pam, lost by Don. Awake it is sugar on her cheek.
4. **The trap and the bang:** she baits him with the roll and saves herself; two shadows, an arm up, one pop. The same frame in colour is Mom with a bottle.
5. **Awake she is holding a party blower where the blueprint was,** and it toots.

## Checked against the series

- **Sofía rejects Don.** In the dream he ends up with her number only because she wrote it for someone else. It is her picture of him: one step behind her, and sure he has solved it.
- **Don approves instead of investigating.** He has a magnifying glass and closes the case on the first thing he sees.
- **Pam fixes things, loves Don, and keeps quiet.** She tidies the street, and what she tidies is the trail that leads Don to that dress.
- **The robot is technically true and needs approval for money.** It cannot keep a coin nobody has approved.
- **The Client is not an idiot and is never hurt.** He wants his drawing, he asks with a sketch, and one cut after the pop he is fine.
- **Mom is used sparingly,** and shouts only inside the dream.
- **The blueprint stays lost.** It is whole only in her dream; nothing is carried into episode 10.

## What it must not become

- **The powder is face powder.** It leaves a compact in a puff, on the street, and becomes footprints. It is never on a table or the bar and never in a line.
- **The diamonds are an idea, not a likeness.** Sofía keeps her own face and hair. No real person and no film is copied.
- **The dress is an evening gown, not a stage costume.** She never dances and the room never gets her attention; the status is hers.
- **The rich men are hands and shirt-fronts.** No new faces to design, and nobody to wonder about.
- **No brands:** the taxi has a roof light and a checker stripe, nothing else.

## For you to decide before the script

1. **Her gloves:** red like the dress (mine), or white.
2. **Pam's line:** "Some dream. Others sweep." (mine: the shape of her line in The Onboarding). Or "Happy New Year. Lift your feet." Or she says nothing and only sweeps.
3. **The tube clutch as the thing he reaches for on the street.** The other way is to cut the reach, so the napkin is the first time we learn what he wants.
4. **Don closing the case on her phone number.** He ends up with it in the dream; awake nothing has happened.
5. **Whether Sofía still says "Of course I did." aloud,** now that Pam has the last line before it. I would keep one of the two.
