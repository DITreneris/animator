## What the film is about

Sofía told the Client "Everything you need is in there" and gave him her CV with her number and a telephone drawn in lipstick. Nobody has his blueprint, and Don does not know about the CV. On New Year's Eve she falls asleep at the party and dreams she is the star the whole town wants, running from the two men who could find her out.

## The protect list (gate G1)

1. **Her narration plays the star; the picture shows her running.** Twice: from the Shadow's hand, and from Don's eye.
2. **The napkin has two sides.** His question, ten bays; her answer, the lipstick telephone. She is caught by her answer, and nobody but her reads the question.
3. **The evidence she leaves:** the wrap, the white footprints, the number. Pam removes one, Don collects two.
4. **The trap and the bang:** the roll makes her a star again for ten seconds; then two shadows, an arm up, one pop. The same frame in colour is Mom with a bottle.
5. **Awake she is holding a party blower where the blueprint was,** and Don gives her a thumbs-up.

## Rules of staging

- **The dream is black and white with one red:** her dress, her gloves, her lips, and what she writes. Nothing else has colour until she wakes.
- **The Shadow never has a face.** Hat brim, collar, gear pin, the watch, one lit hand. He is the only one in the dream who is always behind her, screen left.
- **The same reach three times:** his hand comes in from the left, goes past her, and ends on a thing: the clutch (2.4), the napkin (5.4), the roll (6.6). 2.4 and 6.6 are the same framing, and the frame tilts only in those two shots.
- **Circles see and misunderstand:** her compact, his magnifying glass. Pam and the Bartender look with their own eyes and understand.
- **Each character has a way of being filmed.** Sofía: the camera moves with her, a little below her eye. Don: through his glass, or with the thing he is missing in the same frame. Pam: square on, still. The robot: dead centre.
- **The narration plays the star, and gets shorter as her fear grows** (v4: eleven lines, the longest first). It stops twice: after "He sent a note" she is caught, and says nothing until the office; there she speaks twice more, the second only three words. Under the desk there is no voice at all. Narration is white italics at the top, under the app's bar.
- **New Year's Eve is in the picture from the first frame:** a clock running down to midnight from scene to scene, lights strung in the windows, a banner. The clock strikes twelve on the pop.
- **Holds are protected:** the untouched napkin (5.7), his eye on her (5.9), the wall before the pop (7.2), the toot (8.3).

## What it must not break

- **Nothing is carried into episode 10.** Awake, nobody mentions a CV or a drawing. Don knows nothing.
- **The Client is never hurt.** At the pop no weapon is drawn and one cut later he is fine. *v4: the gang war in 1.2 has guns, shots heard far off, and a shadow that falls. That goes against what this rule was for (the pop as the only bang, nobody hurt). The shots are kept quieter than the pop. Decide whether the fallen shadow stays.*
- **The powder is face powder:** from a compact, in a puff, on the street. Never on a surface in a line.
- **Mom shouts only in the dream.** Awake she is sweet, and she holds the bottle.
- **Sofía keeps her own face and hair.** The gown and the diamonds are a costume on her; no real person is copied.
