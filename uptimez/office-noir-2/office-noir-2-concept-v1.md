# Office Noir 2 — concept v1

UptimeZ Inc. · New Year special · Sofía's dream · for review · nothing is built from this yet

Written from your outline of 17 Nov and your four answers: the Shadow wants his blueprint; she finds it whole; the shot is two shadows on the glass and the real bang is a champagne cork; Sofía narrates, about 60 seconds, black and white with one red.

**Working title:** *Office Noir: The Shadow of a Client*. Two others to choose from: *Office Noir: The Lady and the Blueprint*, *Office Noir: Midnight for One*.

**Target length:** about 60 seconds, eight beats, 9 lines of narration and 6 spoken lines. Real timing comes from the voice takes.

## The idea in one line

On New Year's Eve Sofía dreams she is the woman every noir has: the one who is followed. She is sure the shadow behind her wants her. It wants its drawing back.

**Two wants that collide:** Sofía wants to be wanted, and to be the one holding what everybody needs. The Shadow wants the plan of his ten garages, and thinks she has it.

**One mechanism:** her narration reads every move he makes as desire; the picture shows what his hand is actually reaching for.

**One image:** two shadows on the frosted glass of the office door, a small one with its arm raised and a big one whose hat is falling, and in the dark in front of them one red mouth, open.

**Its twin, awake:** the same composition in colour. Mom holds a smoking champagne bottle. The cork has knocked the Client's paper hat off. He is fine.

## The protect list (first draft, for G1)

1. **She is sure he wants her; the picture shows he wants the plan.** Every beat of the dream carries both.
2. **The napkin:** he draws ten boxes and a question mark, she answers with her number in lipstick.
3. **The two shadows on the glass,** Mom's arm going up. Nothing else of the shot is ever shown.
4. **The cut from the bang to the cork,** one composition, black and white to colour.
5. **What she found is a party blower.** The blueprint is whole only in her dream.

## How it looks and sounds

- **Picture:** as the first *Office Noir*: black and white, rain, hard light through blinds. One red, hers: lips, nails, and what she writes. The noir office set already exists; the street and the bar are new. The party is the ordinary office in colour.
- **Narration:** Sofía's voice from the first film's audition (two semitones lower, with a pause), low and dry. It stops at the shot.
- **Music:** the jazz-noir cue from the first film through the dream, cut by the bang. At the party, *Auld Lang Syne* on the soft vibes you chose today; the tune is traditional and free to use.

## What it must not break

- **The blueprint stays lost.** In the series it is confetti, and only the audience and Pam know. Sofía dreams from what she saw: he gave Don a drawing he keeps losing. Awake, nobody learns anything.
- **The Client is never hurt.** The gun exists only as a shadow in a dream; no body, no wound, and one cut later he is fine and amused.
- **Mom is used sparingly.** Here she shouts only inside Sofía's dream, which is Sofía's picture of her. Awake she is sweet and holds the bottle: silent power, as the bible has her.
- **Nothing is carried into episode 10.** This is a special, like the first *Office Noir*.

## Risks I can see now

| Risk | Why | What I would do |
|---|---|---|
| It needs the narration | A dream told in voice-over can be unreadable on mute, and every episode has to work on mute | Each beat has an action that says it: her lipstick when he is near, his hand going past her, the napkin, the party blower. Test the storyboard on cold viewers before any animation |
| Six scenes in a minute | The first film had one room and ran 39 s | The street and the follow are one set; the bar is one angle; hiding and the shadows share the office. If it runs long, the follow (02) folds into the street |
| The Shadow has to be the Client without a face | Nobody may wonder who he is | His hat brim, the jacket collar, the gear pin and the watch catch the light; his photograph is on the office wall in 04; awake he stands where the shadow stood |
| Mom with a gun | The tone can tip from comedy to violence | Shadows only, a handbag-sized shape, the hat falls and nothing else; the very next image explains it as a cork |
| It repeats the first film's shape | Dream, then Mom's voice wakes the dreamer | That is the form of an *Office Noir*. The difference is who is wrong: Don dreamed he was in charge; Sofía dreams she is wanted |

## For you to decide before the script

1. **The title.** One of the three above, or yours.
2. **The Client's line when she wakes.** "Good shot." (to Mom, about the cork) keeps him easy and unharmed. Or nothing: he only rubs his head.
3. **Don, Pam and the robot in the dream.** I have left them out, so the dream has four figures. Pam could be the one sweeping the bar, with no line.
4. **A second colour.** One red is what you chose. His blueprint could be the one blue, so the eye follows what he wants and what she wants. I would keep it to red in the dream and let the blue arrive only with the colour of the party.
5. **Where it sits in the calendar.** After episode 9, on 31 December, outside the numbering.
