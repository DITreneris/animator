## The idea in one line

On New Year's Eve Sofía dreams she is the star every man in town wants. The only one she chooses wants his drawing back.

**Two wants that collide:** Sofía wants to be wanted, and to choose. The Shadow wants the plan of his ten garages, and thinks she has it.

**One mechanism:** her narration keeps her the star; the picture tells the truth. She says she gives them something to chase, and we watch her run. She says he sent a note, and we see ten garages. She says she waits lower down, and she is under a desk.

**Why the new figures help it:** each one is a way of wanting her, or of not being impressed. The rich men offer money and she does not look. Detective Don follows her trail. The robot is the only one who gives something back. Pam is the only one who cleans up after her. The Shadow is the only one she runs from, and the only one she answers.

**One image:** two shadows on the office wall, a small one with its arm raised and a big one whose hat is lifting, and under the desk a red dress and a red mouth.

**Its twin, awake:** the same frame in colour. Mom holds a smoking champagne bottle; the Client's paper hat is in the air. He is fine.

## The protect list (gate G1)

1. **Her narration keeps her the star; the picture shows a woman running from a man who wants a roll of paper.** His hand always goes for an object: the clutch, the wrap, the napkin, the roll.
2. **The napkin:** ten boxes and a question mark; she counts them, turns it over and answers in lipstick.
3. **The powder trail:** spilled when he reaches and she runs, swept away by Pam, lost by Don. Awake it is sugar on her cheek.
4. **The trap and the bang:** she baits him with the roll and saves herself; two shadows, an arm up, one pop. The same frame in colour is Mom with a bottle.
5. **Awake she is holding a party blower where the blueprint was,** and it toots.

## Checked against the series

- **Sofía rejects Don.** In the dream he ends up with her number only because she wrote it for someone else. It is her picture of him: one step behind her, and sure he has solved it.
- **Don approves instead of investigating.** He has a magnifying glass and closes the case on the first thing he sees.
- **Pam fixes things, loves Don, and keeps quiet.** She tidies the street, and what she tidies is the trail that leads Don to that dress.
- **The robot is technically true and needs approval for money.** It cannot keep a coin nobody has approved.
- **The Client is not an idiot and is never hurt.** He wants his drawing, he asks with a sketch, and one cut after the pop he is fine.
- **Mom is used sparingly,** and shouts only inside the dream.
- **The blueprint stays lost.** It is whole only in her dream; nothing is carried into episode 10.

## What it must not become

- **The powder is face powder.** It leaves a compact in a puff, on the street, and becomes footprints. It is never on a table or the bar and never in a line.
- **The diamonds are an idea, not a likeness.** Sofía keeps her own face and hair. No real person and no film is copied.
- **The dress is an evening gown, not a stage costume.** Strapless, long, with the gloves. She never dances and the room never gets her attention; the status is hers.
- **The run is a comedy run, not a horror one.** He is never a threat: the very next thing his hand does is shake out a shawl looking for a drawing.
- **The rich men are hands and shirt-fronts.** No new faces to design, and nobody to wonder about.
- **No brands:** the taxi has a roof light and a checker stripe, nothing else.
