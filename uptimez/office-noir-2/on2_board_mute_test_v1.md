# Office Noir 2 — cold mute test of storyboard v1

17 Nov · storyboard v1 (41 frames, script v2, the locked cast) · two fresh viewers who saw only the numbered frames

## The intended reading, written before the test

1. **The street.** A glamorous woman in red arrives by taxi at night. A man in a hat follows her. She is flattered until his hand goes for the thing she carries, not for her; she drops her compact and runs, losing her wrap.
2. **The trail.** She leaves white footprints. A sweeper sees a detective coming and sweeps the prints away, so he loses the trail. At a door a robot hands her coin back: it needs approval.
3. **The bar.** Men wave money at her; she hides from the detective behind it. A man's hand sends her a drawing of ten boxes and a question mark. She answers with a phone number in lipstick; he does not take it. The detective finds the number, sees her, and closes the case. She is caught.
4. **The office.** She finds the same drawing rolled in a drawer, and uses it to bait the man in the hat. Someone comes; she hides under the desk. Two shadows on the wall: a small one raises its arm, a bang, his hat flies off.
5. **Awake.** It was a dream. The same picture in colour is a party: an older woman has popped champagne and a man's paper hat is in the air. She wakes at a table with sugar on her cheek and a party blower in her hands. A man in a paper crown gives a thumbs-up, a woman sweeps confetti, she shuts her eyes.

Pass: both viewers tell back at least 4 of the 5.

## Result: fail, strictly

Each viewer read three beats whole and two by half. The same two halves were missed by both, so they are staging faults.

One caveat: viewer A reported that the repo's rules file had loaded into its context and names the series' characters. It used no names and guessed none of the plot from it (neither viewer read the grid as garages), but the viewers were not perfectly blind.

## What read without help

The heel out of the taxi. The hat in her mirror, and her smile. His hand going for the roll, and her run. The sweeper against the detective. The giant eye. CASE CLOSED. The same drawing turning up in the office. The beckon. Under the desk. The cut from the two shadows to the champagne, which one viewer chose as the moment to screenshot and the other called the best moment. The lamp in the last window as the street from frame 1.

## What this test cannot tell

Whether it is funny, whether the narration lands, and anything about timing or the mix. Those need people, with sound.
