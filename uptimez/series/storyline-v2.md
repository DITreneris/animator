# UptimeZ Inc. — the series storyline (v2)

**99.9% uptime. 0% findability.**

v2 takes in the showrunner audit (17 Nov) and your answers. v1 is kept as `storyline-v1.md`.

## In one line

A polished AI start-up sells a search product that finds nothing. Its CEO can sell anything and examines nothing. The only one who tells the truth is the office robot, which needs a human's approval to act, and the only one who actually finds things is Pam, who never gets the credit.

## The principle

**Everyone gets what they want for one moment, and that success makes someone else's situation worse.**

The episodes that work best already do this:
- *Mandatory Fun*: Pam gets Don; Don gets cuffed.
- *Raises for Everyone*: the company gets its 100%; Pam gets nothing.
- *The Best Candidate*: Mom gets hired; Father comes for Don's job.

From now on it's the test for every script.

## The world

- **UptimeZ Inc.** is a Gen Z-styled SaaS company with LinkedIn manners. The videos are its own confident corporate posts, and the brand is the straight man.
- **UptimeZ Find** is its product: search that returns **0 RESULTS**. Its screen is visible on office monitors even when nobody mentions it.
- **One office:** the cafeteria, the meeting room, and the door everyone bursts through.
- **The visual signature:** one big **green success stamp** beside an obviously disastrous result. The robot's verdicts (APPROVED, ACCURATE, 100% SATISFACTION) all use this stamp.

## Rules every episode keeps

1. **It loops, and it leaves a consequence.** The ending leads back into frame 0, and one fact carries into the next episode (a signed pilot, a lost privilege, an exposed secret).
2. **The audience sees the mistake before Don does.** The laugh is waiting for the obviously wrong decision to be approved.
3. **The robot is correct about facts, not about what to do.** It is never a liar or a villain.
4. **One comic mechanism per episode.** Everyone else serves it: two or three speakers per beat, one silent reaction at most.
5. **Status moves in every scene.** Someone gains or loses power, even for a second. Mom entering is enough to shrink Don.
6. **It works on mute.** Stamps, the 0 RESULTS screen, and key lines written as well as spoken.
7. **Length follows the story:** 40–90 s, every pause earned.
