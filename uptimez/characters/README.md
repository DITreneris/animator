# UptimeZ Inc. character sheets

One model sheet per character (expressions and poses) plus `uptimez-team-lineup.png` for heights. Regenerate with `./uptimez/build.sh sheets`.

Drawing rules: DON is always the tallest and widest; heads are about a third of body height; eyes and mouth carry every expression (no noses); faces are never covered by props; every badge carries the mini UptimeZ mark; LAPTOP never smiles, and its one eye-roll comes after the punchline.
