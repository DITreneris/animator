# UptimeZ E16 — "The Review" · Concept v4

Civil War 4 of 5 · follows *The Double Agent* (E15) · ~66 s · 9:16 · must read on mute

## Premise

Father holds a review to pick who will run the company — but what he is really
looking for is the son he wished he had. He has almost chosen the Client (a man
who works with his hands) when the rejected robot bursts in and shows it is the
only one who actually did the work. Father starts to doubt. The last word
belongs to Don: the only human who can say "Approved."

## Decisions (28 Nov, /grill-me)

- Format: generic talent show (not AGT): Father at a folding table with ✓ / X cards.
- Coffee = Pam's job: Don makes coffee without Pam for the first time in his life. The joke is Don, not the machine. "Approved." is said once at the machine.
- Father's motive: the son he wanted. The Client = hands; Don = throne.
- Outcome: Client's name card on the throne, then the robot twist, then REVIEW · TOMORROW.
- Robot twist comes after the Client card; qualities: reviewed the business, never sleeps (UPTIME 100%), costs $0.
- Early rejection + HR badge to Sofía + oil-drop tear stay; the tear triggers the twist.
- Twist ending: robot sits, "Human approval required."; Don reads it for the first time and stays silent (option A). The robot stands up.
- Pam: two lines — "Did anyone review the business?" and "Some sit. Others stand."
- Don's age is not in the bible, so Mom gets no "You're forty." line.

## Beats

**Frame 0 / seam (0–4 s)**
Don asleep in the throne. The robot pulls a folded card from under the caster, the
throne rolls, Don drops. From habit he lifts the empty cup over his shoulder:
**Don:** "Pam! Coffee." — nobody. Pam stands across the tape holding the MY GARAGES blueprint.

**The audition (4–10 s)**
Father unfolds a cheap folding table and chair, ✓ / X cards.
**Father:** "Executive." Sets the card REVIEW · TODAY.

**Rejections (10–20 s)**
- Sofía sits elegantly in the throne. Father, after a pause: X.
- The robot spins on its own axis. **Robot:** "That's optimization." X.
  Robot screen: **Position vacant.** (it left HR to apply). Sofía unpins its HR badge and pins it on herself.
  Oil-drop tear. The robot rolls back to the corner — gathering itself, not finished.

**Don's act (20–36 s)**
Don makes coffee without Pam: starts "Pam—", stops himself; filter in upside down.
Panel: **"Safety limit override. Human approval required."** **Don** (not looking): "Approved."
The machine shakes and creeps toward the counter edge; needle into the red.
On the hiss the Client drops flat under the counter by reflex, hands up (mechanic, "Off the car." from E13).
Don serves Father a cup of black sludge. Father doesn't touch it.

**Almost decided (36–42 s)**
**Don:** "So… who passed?"
Father props the Client's name card on the throne, leans to him under the counter:
**Father:** "Good hands."
**Pam** (to Father): "Did anyone review the business?"

**Twist — the robot (42–54 s)**
From the corner: **Robot:** "Yes." It rolls to Father's table. Three mute beats:
1. Reviewed the business — a long report scrolls from its chest onto the floor. Father reads it; the only one reading anything.
2. Never sleeps — screen **UPTIME 100%**; cut to Don yawning.
3. Costs $0 — screen **SALARY $0**; Father's eye goes to the $49,999 tag on the throne.
Father takes the Client card off the throne: card in one hand, report in the other. Doubt.

**Human approval required (54–62 s)**
The robot sits in the throne. Screen: **"Human approval required."** (the same line that set the bomb).
Everyone turns to Don. For the first time in his life Don reads what he is approving. He says nothing.
No approval — the robot stands up.
Father flips the card to REVIEW · TOMORROW, folds his chair, leaves with the report and the Client card.
**Pam** (everyone standing, the throne empty): "Some sit. Others stand."

**Loop (62–66 s)**
Don folds REVIEW · TOMORROW, wedges it under the caster, sits, falls asleep.
In the dark the robot's screen glows **UPTIME 100%**. It reaches for the card → frame 0.
(Second viewing reveals what was under the caster.)
