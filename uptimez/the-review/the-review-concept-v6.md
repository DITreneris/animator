# UptimeZ E16 — "Approved." · Concept v6 (working title was "The Review")

Civil War 4 of 5 · follows *The Double Agent* (E15) · ~64 s · 9:16 · must read on mute

## Premise

Father holds a review to pick who will run the company, but what he is really
looking for is the son he wished he had. He has almost chosen the Client (a man
who works with his hands) when the rejected robot comes back with the report
nobody let it present. Father starts to doubt. The robot sits in the throne and
needs one human's approval. Don, the only one who can give it, finally reads
what he is approving and deliberately says nothing.

**The spine:** the same HUMAN APPROVAL REQUIRED twice. First Don approves danger
without reading; at the end he reads, understands, and withholds a good
decision to keep his chair. Every beat builds toward that silence.

## Beats

**Frame 0 / seam (0–4 s)**
Don asleep in the throne, tilted back, his weight on it; a folded card wedges the front caster.
The robot pulls the card out. The throne rolls back and Don slides off onto the floor.
Flat on his back, eyes still shut, he raises the empty cup straight up, like a baby bird:
**Don:** "Pam! Coffee."
Across the tape Pam, holding the MY GARAGES blueprint, looks at the cup and turns to the next page.
Her mustard mug sits forgotten on the counter by the coffee machine.

**The review (4–8 s)**
Father unfolds a cheap folding chair beside the gold throne.
**Father:** "Executive." It wobbles. Without being asked, the Client kneels, takes a washer
from his pocket and fixes it. Father sits, tests it: solid. He glances at the Client. Card: REVIEW · TODAY.

**Sofía (8–11 s)**
Sofía sits in the throne, chin up, legs crossed. Father looks past her before she has finished sitting.
She finishes sitting anyway, then gets up.

**The robot (11–17 s)**
The robot rolls up holding out a report. Father raises a hand before it can present.
The robot slowly retracts the report. Screen: **POSITION VACANT** (it left HR to apply).
Sofía unpins its HR badge, pins it on herself and points it to the corner.
One oil-drop tear. Then the screen goes flat: **NOTED.** It rolls to the corner.

**Don's coffee (17–29 s)**
Don will show he doesn't need Pam: he makes the coffee himself for the first time.
1. Filter in upside down. He tries to hand the filter to the Client, sees Father watching, takes it back.
2. Panel: **HUMAN APPROVAL REQUIRED.** **Don** (not looking): "Approved."
   The machine shakes and starts creeping toward the counter edge; the needle starts to climb.
   On the hiss the Client drops flat under the counter by reflex, eyes shut, and without looking
   catches Pam's mustard mug as it slides off the edge.
3. The coffee drops into the cup in one tar-like glug. Don plants a spoon in it; the spoon stays upright.
   He sets it by Father. Hold: Father doesn't touch it.

**Almost decided (29–36 s)**
Father props the Client's name card on the throne. Don, petty, nudges it crooked with one finger;
Father straightens it without looking. He leans to the Client under the counter, mug still in hand:
**Father:** "Good hands."
Don looks down at his own hands.
**Pam** (to Father): "Did anyone review the business?"

**The robot returns (36–50 s)**
From the corner: **Robot:** "Yes." It rolls up and the report it was refused unfurls from its chest,
accordion-folding onto the floor over Don's shoes; Don shuffles back a step. Father reads it.
1. Screen **UPTIME 100%** → Don, eyes closing mid-yawn, head nods, jerks awake. Hold.
2. Screen **SALARY $0** → Father straightens in his cheap chair → his eye drops to the **$49,999** tag
   under the throne's armrest → to Don, smiling proudly, who thinks Father admires the chair. Hold.
Father takes the Client's card off the throne: card in one hand, report in the other. Doubt.
(In the background the needle keeps climbing; nobody looks.)

**Human approval required (50–60 s)**
The robot sits in the throne. Screen: **HUMAN APPROVAL REQUIRED** (same words, same type as the machine).
Everyone turns to Don. Push in on his eyes: for the first time he reads what he is approving.
His mouth opens for "Approved." He sees the robot in his throne. He closes it and folds his arms.
Two full seconds of silence. Sofía adjusts her new HR badge.
The robot sits dead still a beat, then rises slowly and stiffly out of the throne, formal to the last.
Father flips the card to REVIEW · TOMORROW, folds his chair and leaves with the report and the Client's card.

**Loop (60–64 s)**
Don folds REVIEW · TOMORROW (the same fold and orientation as frame 0), wedges it under the front caster,
sits, leans back with his weight on the throne, folds his arms and falls asleep.
In the dark the robot's screen glows **UPTIME 100%**. It reaches for the card → frame 0.
(Second viewing: the card under the caster was REVIEW · TOMORROW.)
