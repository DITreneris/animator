# UptimeZ E16 — "The Review" · Concept v5

Civil War 4 of 5 · follows *The Double Agent* (E15) · ~66 s · 9:16 · must read on mute

## Premise

Father holds a review to pick who will run the company — but what he is really
looking for is the son he wished he had. He has almost chosen the Client (a man
who works with his hands) when the rejected robot bursts in and shows it is the
only one who actually did the work. Father starts to doubt. The last word
belongs to Don: the only human who can say "Approved."

## Beats

**Frame 0 / seam (0–4 s)**
Don asleep in the throne. The robot pulls a folded card from under the caster, the
throne rolls, Don drops. From habit he lifts the empty cup over his shoulder:
**Don:** "Pam! Coffee." — nobody. Pam stands across the tape holding the MY GARAGES blueprint;
her mustard mug sits abandoned on the counter by the coffee machine.

**The audition (4–9 s)**
Father unfolds a cheap folding chair next to the gold throne.
**Father:** "Executive." Sets the card REVIEW · TODAY on the tape and sits.

**Rejections (9–17 s)**
- Sofía sits elegantly in the throne. Father gives her one glance and looks past her. She gets up.
- The robot spins on its own axis by the throne. **Robot:** "That's optimization."
  While it spins, Sofía unpins its HR badge and pins it on herself.
  It stops. Father has already looked away. Robot screen: **Position vacant.**
  One oil-drop tear. Then the screen goes flat: **NOTED.** It rolls back to the corner.

**Don's act (17–29 s)**
Don shows he doesn't need Pam: he makes coffee himself for the first time in his life.
1. Filter in upside down.
2. Panel: **"Safety limit override. Human approval required."** **Don** (not looking): "Approved."
   The machine shakes and creeps toward the counter edge; needle into the red.
   On the hiss the Client drops flat under the counter by reflex — and on the way down his hand
   catches Pam's mustard mug as it slides off the shaking counter. Not a drop spilled.
3. Don serves Father a cup of black sludge. Hold: Father doesn't touch it.

**Almost decided (29–36 s)**
**Don:** "So… who passed?"
Father props the Client's name card on the throne. Don, petty, nudges it crooked with one finger;
Father straightens it without looking. He leans to the Client under the counter, mug still in hand:
**Father:** "Good hands."
**Pam** (to Father): "Did anyone review the business?"

**Twist — the robot (36–50 s)**
From the corner: **Robot:** "Yes." It rolls to Father. Three mute beats, each held a little longer:
1. Reviewed the business — a long report scrolls from its chest onto the floor. Father reads it; the only one reading anything.
2. Never sleeps — screen **UPTIME 100%**; cut to Don yawning. Hold.
3. Costs $0 — screen **SALARY $0**; Father's eye drops to the **$49,999** tag dangling under the armrest. Hold.
Father takes the Client card off the throne: card in one hand, report in the other. Doubt.

**Human approval required (50–60 s)**
The robot sits in the throne. Screen: **"Human approval required."** (the same line that set the bomb).
Everyone turns to Don. Push in on his eyes: for the first time in his life Don reads what he is approving.
Two full seconds of silence. He says nothing.
No approval — the robot stands up.
Father flips the card to REVIEW · TOMORROW, folds his chair and leaves with the report and the Client card.

**Loop (60–66 s)**
Don folds REVIEW · TOMORROW, wedges it under the caster and sits back down on the throne.
**Pam** (as he sits): "Some sit. Others stand."
Don folds his arms and falls asleep. In the dark the robot's screen glows **UPTIME 100%**.
It reaches for the card → frame 0. (Second viewing reveals what was under the caster.)
