# Rough v3

`bp_rough_v3.mp4` is 400 s, 12000 frames, 1920×1080, bt709, −15.3 LUFS, peak −1.1 dBTP.
`bp_rough_v3_nonight.mp4` is 360 s, 10800 frames, the same bed, peak −1.1 dBTP.
Version 1 and version 2 were not rewritten. The four locked stills were not rewritten.

The arrival reads. For the first three seconds of shot 01 he walks out of the glass door to his mark while the frame falls, and the rest of the shot holds him there.

The step back reads. For the first three seconds of shot 04 he walks from his mark toward the door and stays inside the frame. The hold puts him back on the mark, so shot 06 still has him for Friday.
