## What the film is about

He handed over the plan of his ten garages and got a welcome pack with Sofía's CV in it. He paid $999 for Premium and his phone said 100% COMPLETE. The plan is still gone. That night he dreams he is a boxer who knows how business is done. He pays for the fight himself, and what he fights is the machine.

## The protect list (gate G1)

1. **The first drawing is the last:** a glove landing on his face, close.
2. **Every hard line is answered by the picture:** a hit, a bill, or him asking.
3. **He never takes the red gloves off.** So he cannot show a finger, reach his pocket, light his cigarette, hold his money, point, or grip the roll.
4. **He pays for the fight and gets Sofía's CV.** His plan hangs over the ring all night, and he is the only one who looks up.
5. **"Human approval required." Mom: "Approved." The punch.** Awake, the punch is his phone: 100% COMPLETE.

## Rules of staging

- **Black and white with one red: his robe and his gloves.** Sofía's lips are grey this time. Colour comes back only when he wakes.
- **Every shot names its light** (the last column). Shadows stand against a lit wall, never dark on dark.
- **The roll is in every ring shot,** above, in the lamp. Only he looks at it.
- **The punch is framed the same three times:** 1.1 and 8.5 identical; 6.7 wider, so the loop is not given away.
- **Each character has a way of being filmed.** The Client: from a little below, the camera walking with him. Mom: through glass or from his height, looking down at her, and she still wins. Don: through his glass, with the thing he is missing in the same frame. Pam: square on, still. The robot: dead centre.
- **The robot never speaks.** Its chest screen does. Mom approves without looking.
- **Narration is typewriter cards at the top,** under the app's bar. Spoken lines are bubbles, with tails to a mouth that is on screen.
- **Series canon:** the series' proportions between characters (he is a head taller than Don), feet on the floor, walks with a planted foot.
- **Slapstick, not injury:** stars, a folded face, no blood. One cut later he is a man with a phone on his head.

## What it must not break

- **Nothing is said awake.** He is alone. He does not learn where the plan is.
- **Sofía's call is not answered,** and nobody says "CV". It stays for episode 12.
- **Episode 11 is untouched:** he arrives furious, as written. This is the night before.
- **The robot is technically true:** it never hits without approval, and its screen says only that.
- **No gun is fired.** The punches and the bell are the only bangs.
- **Mom does not shout.** She is quiet and in charge.
- **The Client is cheated, not stupid.** He is the only one who sees the roll.
- **The red is his alone** until the cut to colour.
- **No real person, brand or logo.** The phone screen is UptimeZ Find's own.

## What the storyboard has to prove on mute

Questions for two cold viewers who see frames only:

1. What does the man in red want? Where is it?
2. Why does he hold his glove up at the car? Does the woman driving understand?
3. What do the armed men end up doing for him, and why couldn't he do it himself?
4. Who pays whom at the car window? What does he get for it?
5. Whose picture is in the folder? Is it what he wanted?
6. Why doesn't the robot hit him straight away? Who lets it?
7. What does the man with the magnifying glass take, and what does he miss?
8. What does the woman in his corner do for him?
9. What hits him at the end, in the dream and awake?
10. Who is ringing him, and how do you know? What does he do?
11. What is the last image, and does it lead back to the first?

Pass: both tell back at least 4 of the 5 beats (the street and the car; the alley; the deal; the ring; awake), and both can answer question 1.
