**Fixed:** Don is a person for both viewers, for the first time. She walks on the ground and is there for both.
**Half fixed:** the walks are steps now, but small: "waddle", "shuffle". These figures face us and travel sideways, so a stride has little room; 6.2b needs him to come at the camera, not across it.
**Open, both viewers:** his raised glove by her head looks detached and its meaning is unclear; she is fully hidden behind him for two frames; the rope in 6.2c is a line fixed to nothing and he seems to pass through it; in 6.2b his feet sit on the dark band at rope height.
