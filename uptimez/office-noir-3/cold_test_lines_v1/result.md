# Cold test 61 (21 Nov): "If you want to survive, you need to bargain." after "Just looking." (animatic v21)

Two fresh viewers; 31 frames with the narration cards and bubbles in them, 2.5 a second, from 3.2b to 4.4a (22.4 to 34.6 s). The sound cannot be tested this way.

| Question | Viewer A | Viewer B | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Is there a joke across the four lines | "he boasts about bargaining and naming his price, but the other side names the price and he just pays it" | "he boasts "I name my price" right after meekly paying the full price someone else named" | **read (both)** |
| Does the line fit its pictures | "Partly… "Survive" fits the angry robot… "bargain" only lands on the last two frames" | "Only partly… Over the photo in 16-17 the line means nothing and smothers that beat… would land better starting at 16 or 18" | partly: one line over three shots (the door, his face with her photo, her desk) |
| The alley without a narrator | "The joke reads without narration: a snoop with a magnifying glass, caught at gunpoint, uses the shopper's excuse" | "The gag reads fine silent" | read (both) |
| What weakens the joke | "the bill is already in his glove at 18-19, which tips the answer before "How much?"" | the same | a fault of the picture, there before this change |

Text, both: the card "clips" the bottom of the FIGHTERS plate in 4.1; "One hundred bucks." has no tail in 4.4 and "reads as him repeating her line" (she is out of that frame); "I name my price." is tight against his hood in 4.4 ("across the hooded man's hood and face" for one, "does not cover his face but it is crowded" for the other): he is bigger since v24.

Nothing was changed after this test.
