## What the film is about

In episode 9 he handed over the plan of his ten garages and got a welcome pack with Sofía's CV in it. In episode 10 he paid for Premium and his phone said 100% COMPLETE; the plan is still gone. The night after, he dreams he is a boxer who knows how business is done. In the dream he pays for the fight himself, and the thing he is fighting is the machine.

**The mechanism:** his narration is the hard man. In every picture he pays, asks, or gets hit.

## The protect list (gate G1)

1. **The first drawing is the last:** a glove landing on his face, close.
2. **Every hard line is answered by the picture:** a hit, a bill, or him asking.
3. **He never takes the red gloves off.** So he cannot show a finger, light his own cigarette, count his money, or reach the roll.
4. **He pays for the fight and gets Sofía's CV.** His plan hangs over the ring all night, and he is the only one who looks up.
5. **"Human approval required." Mom: "Approved." The punch.** Awake, the punch is his phone: 100% COMPLETE.

## Rules of staging

- **Black and white with one red: his robe and his gloves.** Nothing else has colour until he wakes.
- **Light comes from somewhere in every shot** (lesson 50): the street lamps, the headlights, the lighter, the one lamp over the ring, the phone. The shadows stand against a lit wall.
- **The roll is in every ring shot,** above, in the light. Only he looks at it.
- **The same punch three times, the same framing:** 01, 06 (wider, so it is not the loop yet), 08.
- **Narration at the top,** typewriter cards under the app's bar, as in the first two films. Spoken lines in bubbles.
- **The robot does not speak.** Its screen does, and Mom approves without looking.
- **Slapstick, not injury:** no blood; one cut later he is a man with a phone on his head.

## What it must not break

- **Nothing is said awake.** He is alone. He does not learn where the plan is, and nobody mentions the CV.
- **Sofía's call is not answered.** Her CV stays for episode 12.
- **Episode 11 is untouched:** he arrives furious, as written. This is the night before.
- **The robot stays technically true:** it never hits without approval.
- **Mom shouts nothing here.** She is quiet and in charge. "DONNIE!" is not used.
- **The Client is not an idiot.** He is cheated, and he is the only one who sees the roll.
- **No real person, brand or logo.** The phone screen is UptimeZ Find's own.

## Risks

- **Length.** Both cold pacing viewers found Noir 2 long at 80 s. Test the animatic before animating.
- **Mom carries a lot.** Canon uses her sparingly; here she has three scenes and the last word.
- **Smoking and drawn weapons** in one scene. Fine for the genre; it may limit where the film is promoted.
- **Sofía has no line and three appearances.** Each must do one job: the look, the photograph, the card.
