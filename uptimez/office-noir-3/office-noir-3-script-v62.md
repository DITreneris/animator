# Office Noir 3: The Last Round. Script note v62 (21 Nov)

Film v28, animatic v24. No line is changed.

## What changed (Tomas: "the punch at the start is very short… why?")

Measured in v27: shot 1.1 was 0.6 s on film (0.7 s in the animatic). The first 0.27 s were the still that joins the loop; the glove landed in 4 drawings (0.13 s) and the white was already rising over it; white from 0.5 s. The hit itself was on the screen for about a tenth of a second. Why: the shot was cut down in the early versions to get to the street quickly, and the loop's first frame had to be the still, so the still ate half of what was left.

Now 1.1 is 1.2 s: 0.1 s of the still, the glove lands by 0.22 s, the hit is held to 0.8 s with a shake, then the white. Frame 0 is the same drawing as the last frame (measured: 0). The punch sound is at 0.19 s.

Not tested on viewers. The same hit at the end (7.5) is still short: the glove comes in for 0.6 s, lands, and the white follows within 0.3 s.
