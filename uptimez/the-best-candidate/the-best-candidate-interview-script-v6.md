## The idea in one line

Don asks every candidate the same question. The first corrects him, the second comforts him, the third commands him.

| Candidate | "What if I made a mistake?" | What it shows | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| The small one | "I'd show you the data." | He can do the job | Rejected: "Too short." |
| The nice macho | "Everybody makes mistakes. It's okay." | He can please the boss, and Sofía | Rejected: "Too distracting." |
| Mom | "I'd make you apologize." | She can control the boss | Hired, by the robot |

The robot hires her because she is the only candidate who can manage Don, and Don is too scared to object. The robot never notices that she is his mother. So the one hire that really is on merit is the one that looks like nepotism, under a poster that says WE HIRE ON MERIT.

## How to read the tables

- **Says** is spoken, and appears in a speech bubble.
- **Thinks** is an acting note for the face. It is never written on screen, and it is not a separate shot.
- **We see** is the action, the prop or the wall.
- **MONITOR** lines are what the BIAS MONITOR shows.

## The clues that she is his mother, in order

1. Don sits bolt upright and hides the doodle the moment she walks in (3.2).
2. "Forty years. One very difficult client." (3.5)
3. The curler she forgot (3.1; Sofía sees it in 3.13).
4. "I'd make you apologize." "Sorry." (3.11, 3.12)
5. "Good boy." (3.16)
6. Mother and son have the same stiff silhouette.

Nobody says "mother" at any point. The first time the audience is told is "DONNIE!" (5.3).
