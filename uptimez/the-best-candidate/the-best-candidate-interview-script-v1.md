# The Best Candidate — interview script v1

UptimeZ Inc. · draft for discussion · nothing is built from this yet

**Target length:** 90 seconds. The times below are targets; the real timing comes from the voice takes.

**One change from the first concept, for you to approve.** In the first concept Don hires his own mother and the robot misses it. In this draft the robot hires her, because she is the only candidate who can manage Don, and Don is too scared to object. The robot still misses that she is his mother. So the one hire in the film that really is on merit is the one that looks like nepotism, under a poster that says WE HIRE ON MERIT.

## The idea in one line

Don asks every candidate the same question. The first corrects him, the second comforts him, the third commands him.

| Candidate | "What if I made a mistake?" | What it shows | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| The small one | "I'd show you the data." | He can do the job | Rejected: "Too short." |
| The nice macho | "Everybody makes mistakes. It's okay." | He can please the boss | Rejected: "Too distracting." |
| Mom | "I'd make you apologize." | She can control the boss | Hired |

## Who wants what

| Person | Shows | Hides |
|---|---|---|
| **Don** | A confident Head of Talent | He wants an assistant who makes him look right, and he is afraid of exactly one person |
| **Pam** | A fair, calm panelist | She knows Don is wrong and likes him anyway |
| **Sofía** | The one doing the job properly | She is working out what is really going on |
| **HR robot** | Perfect impartiality | Nothing. It follows its rules and cannot see what they leave out |
| **The small one** | Three degrees and a fixed smile | He knows he is the right answer and that it will not help |
| **The nice macho** | Easy confidence, real kindness | Nothing. He means all of it |
| **Mom** | A sweet old lady | Forty years of doing this job for free |

## How to read the tables

- **Says** is spoken, and appears in a speech bubble.
- **Thinks** is what the character is thinking. It is played on the face, not written on screen. The one exception is marked.
- **We see** is the action, the prop or the wall.
- **MONITOR** lines are what the BIAS MONITOR shows.

## The clues that she is his mother, in order

1. Don sits bolt upright and hides the doodle the moment she walks in (3.2).
2. "Forty years. One very difficult client." (3.6)
3. She picks a thread off his collar and he lets her (3.11).
4. The curler she forgot (3.1, noticed by Sofía in 3.12).
5. "I'd make you apologize." "Sorry." (3.14, 3.15)
6. Mother and son have the same stiff silhouette.

Nobody says "mother" at any point. The first time the audience is told is "DONNIE!" (5.3).

## If it runs over 90 seconds, cut in this order

1. The references exchange (3.20 to 3.22).
2. The lapel pull (5.6).
3. "And a half." (1.4) and the cookie Pam slides to Don (2.14).
4. The mirror (section 6).

The repeated question, the two apologies, the scrub and the father are the film. They do not get cut.
