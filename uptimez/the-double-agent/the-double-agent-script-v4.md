## The story chain (cold viewers must tell it back: at least 8 of 10)

1. The bald man is the boss, a person, and he and a robot fight over a gold chair at a yellow line.
2. An older man walks in over the line, tags the chair, and takes the boss's own office; the robot lets go of the chair, and a young woman follows him in, spilling paperclips the robot then picks up.
3. The boss gives the woman a new mug (she drops her old one); then the dirty man takes the gift and she follows it into the meeting; the older woman hides it from him.
4. She goes back in by her own choice, behind his back.
5. He sees his gift on the table in there.
6. He can't cross his own line; the older woman takes a side against him.
7. The woman brings him a paper; he decides she is spying **for him**.
8. She could tell him the truth and doesn't.
9. He lifts his own line to let her back in, and inside the dirty man hands her his plan.
10. Inside they work without him; outside he is back fighting the robot for the chair (the robot only wants the last clip under it).

**Direct questions:** (1) Who is the man in front: a person or something else? (2) What are he and the robot fighting over? (3) Whose office is the glass room, and who sits in whose chair? (4) Why can't he just walk into his office? (5) Whose mug is on the table inside, who gave it to her, and why does it matter? (6) What does he think the woman is doing for him? Is he right? (7) Why does she say nothing? (8) Who gets the blue plan? (9) The video loops: does the end lead into the start? (10) What is the robot doing on the floor, and what are he and the bald man each pulling for at the end?
