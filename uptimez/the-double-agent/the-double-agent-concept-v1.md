## 1. In one line

**Father holds the meeting without Don inside Don's own office, on Father's side of the line Don taped himself. Pam slips in and out, and Mom covers her.**

The title makes viewers expect Sofía. The double agent is **Pam**. She isn't betraying Don: the Client asked for her ("One month. I ask. She finds it.", 13), and **she never lets the customer down**. Her competence pulls against her love for Don (her central contradiction), and her strategic silence is her small darkness.

## 3. The one comic mechanism

**The rule of three, under cover.** Don calls "Pam!" (coffee) from his side. Each time, Pam must cross the tape and pass Mom's desk to get in or out of his office. Each time Mom covers her with a bigger action, and each crossing is faster:

| Crossing | Pam | Mom's cover | Don |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Walks in with the coffee tray, slowly, on tiptoe | Stands up from her desk: her shoulder fills Don's view | Sees only Mom ("Mom?"), sits back |
| 2 | Comes out and goes back in, quicker, with her notepad under the tray | Drops her knitting and the yarn rolls to Don's feet; he looks down | Picks up the yarn |
| 3 | Shoots in, the door barely open | **Steps off the tape** onto Father's side and stands in front of the office glass with her arms folded. **This is her step toward Pam** | Can't see past her |

Two competing desires (STORYLINE rule 3): **Don wants Pam by his side to prove "everyone's with me"; the Client wants Pam in the meeting.** She serves both.

## 9. Lessons carried in (from 14)

- **Whole-board story cold test before any animation.** 14 skipped it and broke at chain points 6–10 (the hand-over 0/3, the crossing 0/3) after its scenes were animated.
- **Each new prop must read without earlier episodes.** HR? failed because it needed episode 12. Here: the badge moves from the robot's chest to Sofía's on screen; the cup is Pam's, shown in her hand first.
- **Every hand-over in shot, with both owners visible:** the badge (Father → Sofía), the tray, the cup (rules-hands §0, owner rule).
- **The door works as in *The Onboarding*:** sliding glass, street behind, 0.15 s open, a thump at the stop.
- **Version budgets** (production process §4): script 4, layouts 3, each motion test 3, film 5.
