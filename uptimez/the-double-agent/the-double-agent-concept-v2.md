## 1. In one line

**Father's meeting without Don takes place inside Don's own office, and Don, sure he has a spy in there, holds the door open for her.**

## 3. The comic mechanism

**Everything Don does to keep control helps Pam serve the other side**, in three crossings that each mean more:

| Crossing | Meaning | Pam | Don |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **An accident** | Brings coffee; the Client pulls her in | Called for the coffee himself |
| 2 | **A deception** | Goes back in with her notepad behind his back | Busy winding Mom's yarn |
| 3 | **Authorized** | Comes out with the minutes; he sends her back | Holds the door for her |

Mom is Pam's accomplice inside the A-story, not a subplot. Her step off the tape (0:33) is what stops Don seeing the truth.

## 5. The tape: convincing, funny, fun

Tomas's condition for v2. Two jokes plus the loop, each with a meaning the other doesn't have.

### 5.1 Why it is convincing (lesson 10: one visible physical rule)

Floor tape lies sticky side down, so a shoe can't stick to the line itself. **The cause is the leftover roll from 14**, lying by the throne with its free end curled up, sticky side up. The viewer must see that it is sticky before the shoe lands: **a strip of the blue confetti from episode 9 (zone A's corner) is stuck to the tail**, and things stuck to it read as sticky to anyone. The roll is drawn at a size that reads (about Don's fist; under 20 px at feed width nothing reads, lesson 201).

Weight: the roll is a heavy industrial roll. It swings like a pendulum and lands with a thud, and the tape stretches before it rips. Every move sounds.

### 5.3 The lean (0:44–0:50): his own rule, obeyed absurdly

Don will not step over his own line, even to reach his own office.
- **The toes stop exactly at the tape's edge** and curl, a beat on its own.
- He **leans across on one foot**, the other leg out behind for balance (an echo of Sofía's one heel over the line in 14, readable without it), and holds the door open with a flat palm. A flat palm, not fingers: lesson 178, the simplest gesture.
- Pam walks in **under his arm**, unhurried.
- The door shuts. With nothing to lean on, he teeters, arms windmilling, past the point of falling onto Father's side. **Mom presses one finger on his forehead and pushes him upright, back to his side**, silent. That is her contradiction: her protection keeps him a child.
- He lands proud, straightens his vest (his signature), salutes the door.

### 5.4 The loop (0:58 → 0:00)

Don steps down to read the REVIEW tag and his shoe comes down on the roll's tail, as at 0:00. **The loop's cut is the shoe-and-tape close-up** (the review's fix): the throne and its tags are out of that frame, so the last drawing can equal frame 0 while the story has moved on.

### 5.5 Not using the tape (to avoid repetition)

- 0:33 (Mom's step) is a shoulder-pad gag: no wobble on the line.
- The yarn crosses the tape but the yarn is the joke.
- **Episode 16 should open on something else**, since 14 opened on the blinds and 15 on the tape (into STORYLINE v3.5).
