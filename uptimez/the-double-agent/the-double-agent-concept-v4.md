| **THE OFFICE** | Behind the glass in zone C (`da_set.WIN`, x 606–884), door at x 896 | **Father** in Don's chair, **the Client** (oily coveralls), **Sofía**; Pam when she is in | The meeting without him: the real business |
| **THE GLASS** | Between them, with blinds (up all episode) | — | **The episode's engine: whoever is on the floor can see in, whoever is inside can see out** |

**The rule of the glass:** every floor shot that faces the office (S7) shows what is happening inside **at the same moment**, without a cut. The viewer can always check the office; Don checks it only when he turns to it. The joke lives in the gap.
