## The spine (two lines that cross)

- **Pam goes up:** carries coffee -> is called in -> takes notes -> is made a "spy" -> sits at the table with the plan.
- **Don goes down:** owns the throne -> loses his chair and office -> gets the small cup -> crowns himself in a window
  -> on the floor -> doorman at his own line -> fights a robot for a chair.
- **The coffee measures it:** who holds the big mug holds the room.
- **The line is why:** Don drew it; it keeps him out; Pam crosses it both ways.

## The four broken links

1. **3.3 -> 3.4: Don's goal has no trigger.** He never sees his mug go into the office, so his walk to the door and
   Mom's block come from nowhere, and the line is not seen as what stops him.
2. **4.1 -> 4.2: the crown has no cause.** He is handed the small cup and then, unprompted, crowns himself.
3. **4.3 -> 5.1 -> 5.2: 5.1 leads to nothing.** A mood shot between two Don shots.
4. **5.3 -> 5.4: a repeat, not a step.** Mom blocks him a second time the same way; nothing has grown since 3.4.

And one missing line: **Pam never chooses.** The hand takes the mug, she is called in, Don names her a spy, the Client
hands her the plan. Every step up happens to her.
