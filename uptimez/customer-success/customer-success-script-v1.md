# Customer Success — script v1 (line by line, with the shot list)

UptimeZ Inc. · episode 11 · from concept v1 (approved by Tomas, 18 Nov) · for review · nothing is drawn from this yet

v1 takes in Tomas's answers to the concept's open questions (18 Nov): **Sofía's CV stays for episode 12** (here she only looks); **Mom stays silent** (her look at Don at the end says more than a line); **the bank's answer stays off screen** (episode 12 opens on it).

**Length:** about 44 s, 28 shots, 9 lines. Real timing comes from the voice takes.

**Staging:** as in episodes 9 and 10: the Client comes in from the left through the glass door; the staff stand on the right and look left; Pam's desk is at the back, right; Mom's desk is just inside the door. A line is spoken in a single, or a two-shot with clear wall for its bubble, bubbles under the title bar (y 96), never over a face. A reaction shares a frame with its cause. **Sizes:** the Client is the tallest and broadest in every shot (stage scales: Client 0.66, Don 0.75, Sofía 0.70, Pam 0.67; never one character rescaled to fit). **The walls are dressed** in every mid shot (the UptimeZ sign, OUR FIRST CLIENT, crooked): no empty top half.

## The protect list (gate G1)

1. **The portrait that holds**, and the leaf that lands on Don instead and stays on him to the last shot.
2. **"FILES FOUND: 0"** in the small print under a green 100%.
3. **The strip from his pocket completing bay 7**, with Pam's thumb on the tape.
4. **"The result was obtained by a human." → "Approved."** → the stamp. The robot tells the truth and stamps the lie; both are technically true.
5. **PAM · FINDS THINGS** on his phone, while Don shakes his hand and Mom looks at Don.
