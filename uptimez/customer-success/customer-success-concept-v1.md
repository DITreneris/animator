## The principle

**The credit goes to the wrong person, in plain sight.** Everyone in the frame can see who fixed it. The report says the AI did, and the robot, which only ever tells the truth, stamps it, because the stamp says "approved", and it was.

## Beat by beat

### Beat 1: The door, and the portrait that holds (0–4 s)

- **Frame 0:** wide, episode 9's camera. Everyone in place; the portrait crooked over the door; through the glass, the Client coming fast.
- **0.4 s:** he slides the door so hard it slams into its stop. The wall shakes. **Everyone, as one, looks up at the portrait.** It swings... wobbles... **and holds.** A beat of relief: Don breathes out and smiles.
- **1.6 s, the joke:** a ficus leaf lets go and lands on Don's bald head. He doesn't notice. **It stays there for the whole episode.**
- **CLIENT**, phone up, **FRI · BANK 9:00**, checking his watch: "Friday. The bank. Nine o'clock."

**Why it works:** the audience of episodes 9 and 10 expects the third fall, and the show cheats them, then pays it somewhere else. The leaf that marked the Client as the newcomer in episode 9 now marks Don, and nobody tells him.

### Beat 2: The report (4–11 s)

- **DON**, sales smile, the leaf on his head: "Your Customer Success Report."
- **Action:** the robot prints it (its signature). A long glossy page: **BLUEPRINT RETRIEVAL**, a big green **100%**, and a line of small print at the bottom.
- **Insert:** the small print: **FILES FOUND: 0**.
- **CLIENT**, not taking it: "I need my plan. Not a report."

### Beat 3: Pam (11–19 s)

- **Action:** at the back, Pam stands up with the taped plan rolled under her arm. She walks the long way round, **past the shredder**, keeping it on her far side (a callback to ep 10's near-shred). Nobody stops talking.
- **Sofía checks who noticed** (her signature): she sees Pam, and she sees the Client see Pam.
- **Action:** Pam unrolls it on the desk between them and holds the corners down. **MY GARAGES**, nine of ten garages pieced together with tape, and **a hole where garage 7 should be**.
- **Reaction:** the Client looks at the hole. Then at Pam. Pam looks at the hole.

### Beat 4: Bay 7 (19–26 s)

- **Action:** the Client pats his pockets (the gesture from ep 9's last shot), and takes out the strip with the red ring: **7**.
- **CU:** he lowers it into the gap. It fits, edge to edge. Pam's thumb presses a piece of tape over the seam (the third thumb of ep 10, finished).
- **The plan is whole.** Hold. No music; the room tone only.
- **ROBOT**, flat: "The result was obtained by a human."

### Beat 5: The credit (26–34 s)

- **Action:** Don, beaming, turns the report toward the robot. A box on it: **FOUND BY: AI**.
- **ROBOT:** "Report change. Human approval required."
- **DON:** "Approved."
- **Action:** the robot stamps **APPROVED** on it. Technically true: it was approved.
- **SOFÍA**, low, watching the Client: "He's not looking at you."
- **Reaction:** Don checks the Client, who is looking at Pam. Don straightens his vest anyway (his signature). The leaf stays put.

### Beat 6: The number (34–44 s)

- **CLIENT**, to Pam: "Your number."
- **Action:** Pam freezes; then, without a word, writes it on the tape across bay 7 (no line: strategic silence). He photographs it and saves the contact. **Insert, his phone:** **PAM · FINDS THINGS**.
- **Reaction, Pam:** one second of being seen. She almost smiles. **Mom looks at Don** (her signature).
- **Action:** Don steps in and holds out his hand; the robot's paddle shows **CONTACT APPROVED**; the Client shakes it without looking at him, eyes on his watch.
- **DON**, to the room: "Customer success."
- **Action:** the Client leaves for the bank, the plan under his arm, and slides the door shut. Gently. The portrait doesn't move.
- **Last frames:** the wide shot of frame 0; at the back, Pam's phone lights up on her desk. The leaf, at last, slides off Don's head as he turns.

**The loop:** the last frame is frame 0's wide shot; the next frame 0 is the Client storming in again. The image loops; the story has moved: **the Client's number is on Pam's phone, and Don has a stamped report that says the AI did it.**

## G1 protect list (the five things that make the episode)

1. **The portrait that holds**, and the leaf that lands on Don instead and stays.
2. **"FILES FOUND: 0"** in the small print under a green 100%.
3. **The strip from his pocket completing bay 7**: the customer does the last step, with Pam's tape.
4. **"The result was obtained by a human." → "Approved."** The robot tells the truth and stamps the lie, both technically true.
5. **PAM · FINDS THINGS** on his phone, while Don shakes his hand.

## G0 series check

| Rule | How |
|---|---|
| Two wants collide | Don wants the credit for the client; the Client wants his plan. Pam's silence is the third force |
| One comic mechanism | Credit given to the wrong person, in plain sight |
| One iconic image | The strip going into bay 7, the plan whole |
| The image loops, the story advances | Same wide shot, same door; the number is now on Pam's phone |
| Status moves | Pam up (seen, and asked for her number); Don down while believing he is up; Sofía reads the room and is the only one who says it |
| Works on mute | The hole, the strip, the 0 under the 100%, the stamp, the phone contact: every turn is a thing you can see |
| The robot is technically true | It says a human did it, and stamps APPROVED because it was approved |
| Canon locks | Find still finds nothing (FILES FOUND: 0); Sofía fakes no record (she only says what she sees); Don believes his own pitch |
