1. **The shredder makes Sofía and Don the shredders.** Both read Sofía as having shredded the blueprint (3.3: she stands by the shredder) and Don as "shoving paper into the shredder" (5.5: the shredder behind his hip with a sheet in its slot). The shredder stays out of every shot that isn't about it.
2. **Three near-identical robot frames (4.4, 5.1, 5.2) are dead air.** 4.4 gets the plan in the bottom of frame and its paddle's green tick (as the script says); 5.2 becomes the robot and Don with the report between them.
3. **The flash (6.6) read as a fade-out**, Don and the robot "made irrelevant". The flash: a white burst from the robot's lens, rays, the figures sharp.
4. **Mom is a stranger** ("who is the granny?"). She is never established before 6.10. She goes into frame 0 at her desk (ASSISTANT TO DON) and 6.10 becomes over her shoulder onto Don, so her look has a target.
