# Daddy's Home: concept v1

UptimeZ Inc. · episode 12 · 21 Nov · concept stage, for review. Nothing is built.

**Logline:** A one-star post goes viral, and Don's father sees it. He is a feared nightclub kingpin and the company's majority investor. He announces a visit for Monday at five, and the whole office spends the episode making a broken business look like a working one. He walks in for the last fifteen seconds and asks for one thing.

**Format:** up to 90 s for now (trim after the full cut), vertical, mostly one location: the front desk of episodes 9–11. One new set: a nightclub booth, used for one short shot.

## Canon this rests on

- **Father:** the investor (majority share; can replace the CEO). Mom knows it. "Father is parking." (ep 8) has been waiting since then. In STORYLINE's words: a threatening version of Don; Mom makes Don feel like a little boy, Father makes *others* see him as one. He is the one character who is not funny on purpose; the comedy is everyone's reaction.
- **One Star's consequence:**
  - the post went viral (2.4K);
  - the company's public reply is "Shut off! Our AI does not lose files!";
  - the review still says one star;
  - the Client saved Pam's number.
- **The calendar:**
  - One Star ends on FRI 12, the call is the same Friday;
  - the pages fall to MON 15 (First Search's falling-page move, the one wall calendar, right of the portrait).
- **The robot:** factual and literal; needs human approval for money, access, commitments and systems.
- **Mom:**
  - silent power;
  - dislikes Sofía;
  - secretly wants Pam as Don's wife;
  - the wet thumb (ep 10).
- **Sofía:** flirts upward; never fakes a record.
- **Pam:** strategic silence; does the real work.

## Risks

1. **Father must not take over the series.** One entrance, few words. The comedy stays the staff's.
2. **Fear is not a joke.** Every panic beat must turn into a new absurd act (the green star, the sign turned to Don, the chair, the honest metrics, the thumb). The review warns the same.
3. **90 s** is long for the feed. It is a working length; the trim comes after the full cut.
4. **The loop and frame 0.** Frame 0 is Don's phone (the joke at 0:01). The club booth comes right after as Father's side of the call. So the episode both shows the bodyguard's post and loops on DAD.
5. **Pam at the end:** she has no line after the break. Option: her look at Don when he says "Yes, Dad." (to decide in the script).
6. **STORYLINE changes:** Father moves from 14 to 12. Episodes 13–15 per the review: 13 The Demonstration, 14 The Refund (the Client returns), 15 Permission to Succeed. To update when the concept is approved.
