## Things to check

1. **"Approved." is said twice** (line 13 by Don, line 31 by Father). That is on purpose: Father takes Don's word from him.
2. **"Donnie" is said twice** (line 1 by Father, line 29 by Mom). Also on purpose: the same name, cold and then kind.
3. **Father says "find" twice in a row** (lines 34 and 35). On purpose: he quotes the product, then uses it.
4. **Sofía has one line.** Her other moments are silent (the REPORTS folder, jumping out of the chair, the CV). If that's too little, the candidate to give back is "You showed the coffee man our numbers?" after line 27, in place of the robot's line 24.
5. **Pam has two lines, both corrections of Don** ("Into what?", "It's still one."). Her real moment is silent, at line 19.
6. **Line 3, "My people come first."** could be heard as "my people matter most". If it reads that way to you, an alternative is "My people arrive first. Five o'clock."
7. **Line 33, "I prefer green in cash."** depends on the viewer remembering the star went green 50 s earlier. The star is on screen while he says it.
8. **Pronunciation for the voices:** "AI" is read as two letters; "CEO" as three letters; "non-functional" as two words; "€80" is on screen only, never said.
9. **Nobody says** "inspector", "repairman", "bodyguard" or Father's name. The pictures carry those.
