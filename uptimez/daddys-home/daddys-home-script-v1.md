## The episode in one line

Don tries to look like a CEO for his father, and everything he touches makes him look less like one.

## The protect list (G1)

1. Father never jokes, shouts, slips or hurries. Eight short lines.
2. "Good. Find my money." and the robot's slow head turn to the chair.
3. The wet filter stays on Don's head until Mom takes it off.
4. "I'm here to inspect the machine." Nobody looks at the coffee machine.
5. The loop: DAD rings while Father stands in front of him. "Answer it."

## The set and who is where

The front desk, v5 (opt-in): the **CEO chair** in zone B under OUR FIRST CLIENT, on casters; the **coffee machine** on the counter in zone C, at Pam's elbow, with an OUT OF ORDER note. FRI 12 on the wall all episode. Home positions as in `series/front-desk-set.md`; the robot at its station by the glass door (zone A).
