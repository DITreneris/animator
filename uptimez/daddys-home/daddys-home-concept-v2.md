# Daddy's Home: concept v2

UptimeZ Inc. · episode 12 · 21 Nov · concept stage, for review. Nothing is built. Concept v1 is kept (`daddys-home-concept-v1.md`).

**Logline:** A one-star post goes viral, and Don's father sees it. He is a feared nightclub kingpin and the company's majority investor, and he is coming **today** at five. Trying to look like a working business, the office mistakes the coffee-machine repairman for Father's inspector and shows him everything. Then the real Father walks in.

**Format:** about 90 s for now (trim after the full cut), vertical, mostly one location: the front desk of episodes 9–11. One extra set: the nightclub booth, used for one short shot.

## Risks

1. **The repairman must not steal the episode** (the Gogol review's own FAIL 1). His chain stays at about 25 s, beats 6 to 9; Father keeps the ending.
2. **About 90 s and about 40 lines** is long for the feed. It is a working length; trim after the full cut. First candidates: beat 2's Pam exchange, beat 5 (Mom's chair) shortened, beat 7's tour.
3. **New props on the set:** the CEO chair and the coffee machine must be planted early (both are in frame 0's wide shot) so beats 3, 5 and 9 pay off something already seen.
4. **The green star:** big, and changing on screen, or it won't read (One Star's lesson).
5. **STORYLINE:** Father moves from 14 to 12. Episodes 13–15 per the first review: 13 The Demonstration, 14 The Refund (the Client returns), 15 Permission to Succeed. To update when the concept is approved.
