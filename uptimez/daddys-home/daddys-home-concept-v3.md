# Daddy's Home: concept v3

UptimeZ Inc. · episode 12 · 21 Nov · concept stage, for review. Nothing is built. v1 and v2 are kept as they are.

**Logline:** A one-star post goes viral, and Don's father sees it. He is a feared nightclub kingpin and the company's majority investor, and he is coming **today** at five, his people first. Trying to look like a working business, the office mistakes the coffee-machine repairman for Father's man and shows him everything. Then the real Father walks in, and the robot looks at the chair.

**Format:** about 90 s, vertical. The front desk of episodes 9–11, plus one shot of the nightclub booth.

**Rule for this draft (from the review):** every big verbal joke has a visible action that causes it or makes it worse.

## Risks

1. **The repairman mustn't take over.** His chain is beats 6–9, about 19 s now; Father keeps the ending.
2. **Length:** about 90 s, about 38 lines. Trim after the full cut. First candidates: beat 2's Pam exchange, beat 5 shortened.
3. **Fast hits** (the pop, the filter landing, the chair rolling back): each is under 0.5 s, so they need the 8 fps sheet in the mute test.
4. **Continuity:** the filter stays on Don from 0:56 until Mom takes it off at about 1:04. Every shot in between needs it.
5. **The robot's head turn** has to be a slow, big action, held, not a glance. Don's sidestep and the robot's follow are separate moves, one after the other.
6. **The green star:** big, changing on screen.
7. **STORYLINE and CLAUDE.md** still say Father arrives in episode 14. To update once the concept is approved (12 Daddy's Home; 13 The Demonstration; 14 The Refund; 15 Permission to Succeed).
