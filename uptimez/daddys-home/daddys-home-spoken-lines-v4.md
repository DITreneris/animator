## Things to check

1. **"Approved." is said twice** (line 13 by Don, line 31 by Father). On purpose: Father takes Don's word from him.
2. **"Donnie" is said twice** (line 1 by Father, line 29 by Mom). On purpose: the same name, cold and then kind.
3. **Father says "find" in two lines running** (34 and 35). On purpose: he quotes the product, then uses it.
4. **"CEO" is now said twice** (line 24 by the robot, line 28 by Don). The robot's ruling sets up his protest.
5. **Line 3 and the repairman.** Father's "men" are his bodyguards. Don takes the first big man in black for one of them. Nobody explains this; the bodyguards walking in first at 1:08 is the answer.
6. **Sofía has one line** (7). Pam has three (10, 14, 27b).
7. **Line 33, "I prefer green in cash."**, kept. The green star is on screen while he says it.
8. **Pronunciation for the voices:** "CEO" as three letters; "$80" is on screen only, never said.
9. **Pam's remarks so far:** "Some shit, others scoop." (9) · "Some post. Others tape." (10) · "Some post. Others find." (11) · "Some inspect. Others drink." (12).
