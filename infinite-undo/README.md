# Infinite Undo (shelved)

ME + MY AI concept, shelved on 16 Nov 2026 after the key poses (`key_poses.png`, `python3 stills.py sheet`).

Hoody spills his water, MY AI rewinds it with the UNDO button on its chest, and the cat learns to work the button until Hoody becomes its drinking fountain.

## Why it was shelved

It is a good gag but not a ME + MY AI episode. Checked against what made Perfect Date and Perfect Trip work:

| Check | Infinite Undo |
|---|---|
| What Hoody wants | a glass of water: nothing at stake |
| The AI takes it | no: the cat is the agent, MY AI is a tool |
| Ironic surface | none: slapstick on slapstick |
| One iconic image | a rewinding water arc that reads like a tentacle |

And the joke is a mechanism the viewer has to decode: visual clarity scored lowest in all four outside audits.

## Lesson

**Score a concept on the four checks above before drawing anything.** Four rounds of audits polished how the joke works; none asked whether it had the series engine.

## Kept for later

- The fountain as a possible short cat end-tag.
