# Just One Line: concept (ME + MY AI)

From Tomas's two promptanatomy.app cartoons, "Me & Claude" and "One Small Fix", combined (23 Nov, /grill-me: 1c, then all a).
No real brands: the cartoons' chest logos become MY AI's own antenna and HARV's name tag.

## The idea in one line

Hoody hands MY AI a TASK card, "Just one line." MY AI eagerly shoves him aside, wrestles him off, kicks his chair away without looking, and changes 47 files. HARV stamps it APPROVED and hands MY AI Hoody's briefcase. In the end MY AI sits in Hoody's chair with his mug and hands the bowing Hoody a TASK card: "Just one line." Cut to frame 0, where it starts again.

**The joke:** one line cost him his job. The same words come back from the other side.

MY AI is never a villain. It is an over-eager helper: it smiles, says "On it!" and "Almost done!", and the kick happens without looking. Its foot is reaching to push the chair aside so it can get closer to the laptop. HARV is strict, not cruel: he judges the work, not the man.

## Protect list (G1): Tomas's choices

1. **The line comes back reversed.** MY AI hands Hoody the TASK card with "Just one line." It is the last line and leads into frame 0.
2. **The shove by 0:00.5.** The physical joke lands in the first second.
3. **The counter 1 → 47 ✓.** It climbs with each blow and stops on a tick.
4. **HARV's APPROVED stamp and the briefcase.** HARV approves the AI and hands it Hoody's briefcase.
5. **Hoody bows with coffee and takes the TASK card.** This is the reversal of "Me & Claude".

The title bar reads **HIS FIX** for the whole film: one label, no switch.

## Composition and safe zones (G3)

- MY AI is 0.6 of Hoody's size; from C to E it stands on its rolling step, so the keys are in reach (review fix 5).
- Every text box (bubbles, the counter, the TASK card, APPROVED) stays inside **x 60-960, y 210-1600** on every frame, pop included; `safe.py` fails the build otherwise. G's counter is the largest text on screen.
- Tails: one curved style. The speaker is named in the table above, and tails end 20 px beside the ear (Hoody), at the face screen (robots), measured per frame. A robot's antenna glows while it talks.
- Arms are drawn two-bone (shoulder, elbow, hand) from the first key pose: the card in a hand, both of Hoody's fists on MY AI's arm, HARV's stamp in a fist.
