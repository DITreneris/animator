## Source and pattern

Four motivational YouTube Shorts Tomas sent (descriptions only): *Discipline mindset*,
*You can do that. Stay consistent*, *Overthinking, Motivation, Discipline, Ego*, *Get out of your comfort zone*.

The genre arc: split frame with labels → obstacle or doubt (chasm, heavy stone, "You can't") →
step-by-step effort (jump, stacking, pull-ups) → reframe: what looked like losing leads to a yellow SUCCESS.

The Hoodyz flip: Hoody walks beats 1–3, the beat-4 success goes to MY AI (HIS → ITS).

- Take: two or three lanes in the 9:16 frame; one physical metaphor per episode; labels on objects and characters (YOU, FAIL); a loop through a repeated action.
- Don't take: a compilation of unrelated metaphors; a serious moral at the end; the sources' drawings, figures, brand or layout; licensed music.

## Criteria (1–5 each)

K1 what Hoody wants · K2 the AI takes it · K3 ironic surface · K4 iconic image (×1.5) · K5 a witness (Eva) ·
K6 loop · K7 reads without sound · K8 production cost (×0.5) · K9 freshness. Max 45. Scores are judgement, not platform data.
